04 · The honest counterargument

Faster tasks.
Not always less work.

55%

Reported the same working hours.

Among teachers using AI in a 2026 Bett / YouGov survey. Another 4% said they worked more hours.

CheckingReworkMaintenanceMore expectations

Our design rule

Remove work.
Don't multiply it.

Measure the full process: creation + checking + publishing + updating. If that total grows, we change the process.

Real savings = time removed after all new work is counted.05 / 10

07 · Shared expertise, teacher autonomy

Share expertise. Not paperwork.

Subject experts improve teaching; a central trust capability makes the consequences easier to manage.

TRUST AI TEAM

Build once.

Conversion process, templates, accessibility tools, publishing and technical maintenance.

SUBJECT EXPERTS

Keep it accurate.

Approve curriculum content and sequencing, not every design or navigation detail.

SEND + PEDAGOGY

Make it usable.

Agree inclusive design standards, independent-learning expectations and practical evaluation.

The workflow follows existing practice. It does not impose a common PowerPoint or a new teacher reporting process.

08 · Test the entire value chain

Prove the benefit.
Then scale.

Six-week pilot across three departments: workload, curriculum coherence, accessibility and pupil usefulness.

01Baseline

Measure time spent preparing and updating cover, schemes, revision and assessment.

02Compare

Follow a real lesson improvement through all affected documents and pupil-facing views.

03Test

Count checking and maintenance, plus SEND/pupil feedback and technical reliability.

04Decide

Does net staff time fall? Are resources more accurate, current and usable?

Success means better curriculum access and less unnecessary work — not more generated pages.
