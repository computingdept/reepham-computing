## What was completed

- Guided practice follows **Try → Discuss → Improve** rather than a generic independent question page.
- Original source CHECK explanations appear after independent work and the Exit Ticket, not alongside unattempted tasks.
- Original existing pages and embedded diagrams are retained when suitable; the alternate-layout drafts are rebuilt from the original source, with worked diagrams placed after the Exit Ticket.
- Original SVG duplicates for **016, 028, 031 and 205** were verified, ensuring that each distinct static source drawing is included.
- Working links checked for A4 colour and black-and-white and both A3 booklet versions, with page counts checked.
- The final full-course audit flagged no missing section-order markers, PDF page-count discrepancies, missing linked files or early-source-CHECK markers.
- Original interactive classroom lessons, revision guides and website navigation have **not** been modified in this We Do production.

## Quality gates remaining before student distribution

These are genuine remaining editorial checks; they must not be reported as completed merely because the PDF generator passed.

1. **Teacher assessment of subject content and guided practice:** OCR H446 terminology, source code, explanations, correctness and strength of specific discussion prompts.
2. **Visual and diagram accuracy:** inspect source-to-print placement, readability, diagrams, colour/monochrome and possible duplicates. An image-count check does not validate technical accuracy.
3. **Layout review of 33 longer booklets:** determine which can be shortened without losing necessary diagrams, worked steps or writing space. The standard remains **eight A4 pages where educationally sensible**, rather than an absolute cap.
4. **Individual approval before teaching:** all catalogue `teacher_approved` flags remain false. A visible **Print** button means a file is available for teacher review, not approved for classroom delivery.
