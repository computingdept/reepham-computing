**What changed:** adjacent diagram-only PDF panels were recombined with less repeated header/footer area. The current original source-figure pages were reused as PDF crops rather than redrawing diagrams, and every original non-diagram page was preserved.

**What automated checks passed:** exact image raster content and displayed print dimensions, original non-figure PDF text, guided TRY / You Do / Exit Ticket / CHECK order, matching black-and-white/colour page counts and A3 booklet imposition. All figure dimensions remained unchanged. Failed compaction is not forced.

**What has not been signed off:** an independent human comparison of the two preview montages at A4 print scale; legibility and placement of K-map labels, graphs and captions; room to write; accuracy of source material. All pilot resources remain unapproved.

## Recommendation

Only promote the Lesson 106 or 029 pilot to a student-facing Print link after checking their comparison and first printed A4 output. **Do not mass-compress the other 30 long lessons yet.** The same method may help many, but it should be applied selectively; Dijkstra is a clear example where conserving graph legibility is more valuable than saving pages.
