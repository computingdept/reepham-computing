4 · Same curriculum, different access

Accessibility should travel with the learning.

A pupil may need foundational reading support and still need to remain connected to their age-appropriate curriculum. The Hub can provide different ways into the same learning, with additional support routed alongside it where appropriate.

5 · Why the web?

No specialist pupil app.
No second planning system.

The open web gives us a low-friction delivery layer: a current link can work on a school PC, Chromebook, tablet or phone. The student does not need PowerPoint installed simply to access the adapted lesson.

THE ACCESS MODEL

One link. Multiple devices.

Responsive web pages can include read-aloud controls, larger text, alternative backgrounds, one-step views, self-marking practice and downloadable originals.

Open Learning Hub ↗
WHAT STAYS TRUE

The source belongs to the school.

The teacher-created PowerPoint, booklet or worksheet remains the source. The web is an additional access and practice layer — not a replacement for normal teaching.

7 · From school proof to trust infrastructure

Build the building.
Let expertise fill it.

The scalable opportunity is not for one person to create thousands of resources. It is to establish a common architecture, workflow and quality standard so school expertise can accumulate and, where useful, be shared across Synergy.

1 · CREATETeacher expertise

Existing school curriculum and department resources.

2 · ADAPTAI-assisted

Reformat, accessibility, practice and alternative views.

3 · ASSUREProfessional QA

Teacher, SEND and curriculum checks before deployment.

4 · DEPLOYOne learning layer

School use first; trust sharing where it adds genuine value.
