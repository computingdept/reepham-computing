Year 13: recall everything, deepen what is difficult

Assume every OCR H446 specification area will be revisited in Year 13, even if it was taught in Year 12. Simple knowledge is retrieved briefly and repeatedly, not allocated another full teaching lesson. The primary objectives below are taught in depth where the material is difficult, practical, mathematical or frequently weak in examination answers.

Retrieval in every lesson

Do Now: up to four questions. Two short questions from previous content, one link to the current learning, and Q4 as a higher-demand challenge. Answers remain on a separate screen.

Depth where it earns marks

Prioritise unfamiliar algorithms, OOP, debugging, floating point, logic, SQL, processor tracing, complexity, extended evaluation and precise OCR exam notation.

Synoptic links

Each lesson lists a connected prerequisite and an interleaved recall area from elsewhere in the course, including the other paper where useful.

Assessment over re-teaching

Use examiner-report misconceptions, past-paper questions and student self-marking. Re-teach only when a misconception persists.

All specification headings are represented in the recall rotation: 1.1.1–1.5.2 and 2.1.1–2.3.1. The detailed OCR sub-points still determine the objectives of the main teaching lessons; retrieval headlines are not a substitute for that detail.
