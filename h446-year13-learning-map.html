Fortnightly teaching allocation

10 timetabled lessons per fortnight (five per teaching week). This is a provisional sequence of teaching periods, not a claim about completed work or exact timetable days. Numbered lessons point to the titles and objectives below; other sessions are allocated to further specification teaching, practice, assessment or NEA.

Capacity to study leave: 35 + 35 + 30 + 25 + 15 = 140 planned periods across 28 teaching-week equivalents. Summer 1 is capped at 15 periods in the existing hub because of study leave. Autumn 2 includes protected NEA work and December mocks may replace scheduled periods.
