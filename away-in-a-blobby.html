Before we start · costumes and props

Everyone: traditional Nativity role with pink-and-yellow Blobby spots or accessories. Narrators have spotted badges or bow ties; angels have spotted halos; shepherds have tea towels and spotted sashes; wise men have spotted crowns; Mary and Joseph have spotted robes; Star has a Blobby face; innkeeper has a spotted apron. Gabriel has a lightweight Blobby decoration attached safely to a music stand, not obstructing the trumpet.

Onstage: manger with lightweight baby doll and blanket, three gifts, safe spot for trumpet. Offstage: form tutor in full Mr Blobby costume, waiting at side entrance. Someone offstage controls the music and lights.

Safety: Blobby gently lifts and hands the doll to Mary before the group dance. No running, shaking or throwing the doll. All 20 students join the dance, and everyone must be able to see through costumes.
