Rehearsal copy · approximately 4 minutes

Away in a Blobby!

20 students plus form tutor as Mr Blobby. Times are rehearsal targets, so keep spoken lines brisk and cue the music cleanly. Trumpet fanfares should last around 3–5 seconds each.

Cast — exactly 20 students

RoleNumberJob
Narrators2Tell the story and deliver the punchlines
Mary and Joseph2Lead the Nativity and react to Blobby
Gabriel / trumpet1Three fanfares; joins dance after putting instrument down
Innkeeper1Short opening exchange
Wise men3Gifts and jokes
Shepherds5Short lines and group reactions
Angels5Choreographed reactions and dancing
Star of Bethlehem1Leads entrances and dance formation

Props: cardboard manger frontage, blanket, three gifts, shepherds' staffs, simple halos, star, trumpet and Blobby costume.

1 · The Nativity (0:00–0:40)

🎺 TRUMPET: Short, beautiful Christmas fanfare.

NARRATOR 1: Long ago, in Bethlehem, Mary and Joseph were searching for somewhere to stay.

JOSEPH: Have you got a room?

INNKEEPER: Sorry, we're fully booked. There's a stable round the back.

MARY: It'll have to do.

Mary and Joseph move to the manger; angels and shepherds enter.

NARRATOR 2: And that night, something truly extraordinary happened.

GABRIEL: Fear not! I bring wonderful news!

SHEPHERD 1: You could have knocked!

2 · The Wise Men (0:40–1:05)

The three wise men enter with gifts. Other shepherds and angels silently gather round.

WISE MAN 1: I bring gold.

WISE MAN 2: I bring frankincense.

WISE MAN 3: I bring myrrh.

SHEPHERD 2: Nobody thought to bring nappies?

NARRATOR 1: And there, beneath the star, they discovered the most extraordinary sight...

🎺 TRUMPET: Grand fanfare. Freeze for two seconds.

3 · The Blobby Reveal (1:05–1:45)

Blobby rises safely from behind the manger front. Costume has been hidden until this moment.

BLOBBY: BLOBBY! BLOBBY! BLOBBY!

🎺 TRUMPET: One deliberately discordant note!

Everyone freezes in disbelief.

JOSEPH: MARY! WHAT IS THAT?!

MARY: DON'T LOOK AT ME!

WISE MAN 1: I think we've come to the wrong stable.

WISE MAN 2: Can we get the gold back?

Blobby waves and tries to approach Joseph; Joseph steps back behind the shepherds.

NARRATOR 2: This was not quite how the story was supposed to go.

BLOBBY: BLOBBY!

4 · Blobby Chaos (1:45–2:20)

🎵 MUSIC: A short excerpt from the 1993 song Mr Blobby.

Blobby dances badly. Angels recoil. Shepherds attempt to restore order. Wise men back away.

JOSEPH: Somebody do something!

🎺 TRUMPET: Two short notes. Music cuts. Blobby freezes.

NARRATOR 1: Finally, peace returned to Bethlehem.

BLOBBY: BLOBBY BLOBBY BLOBBY!

MARY: Oh, just join in!

Everyone takes their two-row dance positions. Gabriel puts the trumpet on a secure stand or safe place before joining.

5 · Christmas Dance (2:20–3:50)

🎵 MUSIC: Merry Christmas Everyone — Shakin' Stevens.

Two rows of ten, Blobby at front. Repeat this 16-count routine through the chorus:

  1. 1–4: Step right, left; clap twice.
  2. 5–8: Step right, left; hands in the air.
  3. 9–12: March four counts on the spot.
  4. 13–16: Point at Blobby; finish with a clap.

Comedy beat: All students perform the moves correctly; Blobby gets them wrong. Repeat, then everybody joins for the last chorus. End pointing at Blobby.

6 · Final Joke (3:50–4:00)

Music stops. Everyone freezes in a Nativity tableau.

NARRATOR 1: And they all lived happily ever after.

🎺 TRUMPET: One beautiful closing note.

BLOBBY: BLOOOOOOBBBBBYYYY!

EVERYONE: NOOOOO!

BLACKOUT. END.

Rehearsal checklist

  • Pick students for all 20 parts and mark the dance rows.
  • Keep the Blobby costume hidden until the reveal; test sightlines and exits.
  • Test trumpet cues and music volume before the show.
  • Rehearse the switch from sketch to dance with a stopwatch.
  • Use safe walking and comedy reactions: no running into students, lifts or planned falls.
  • The Nativity is the serious setup; the humour comes from Blobby interrupting it.
