Away in a Blobby!

Full rehearsal script — timings, lines and stage actions

4 minutes · 18 performers + 2 backstage crew · form tutor as Mr Blobby

Before 00:00: Of the 20 students, 18 perform onstage: one Narrator, five girl shepherds (Blobby’s supporters), five silent angels, three wise men, Mary, Joseph, Gabriel (trumpet only) and one innkeeper. Two students are backstage crew: one on sound, one on lighting. The baby doll starts in the manger; the form tutor enters as Mr Blobby from the side. Timings are rehearsal targets.
SCENE 100:00–00:40

The Nativity begins

STAGE
All 18 onstage students wear Blobby-themed Nativity costumes. Baby doll lies wrapped in the manger. Mr Blobby waits out of sight at the side entrance. Gabriel, Narrator, Mary, Joseph and the one innkeeper are ready. The two other students are at sound and lighting controls.

SCENE 301:05–01:45

Mr Blobby meets the baby

01:05
BLOBBY — ENTRANCE
The form tutor waddles on from the side in the full Mr Blobby costume. Notice the baby doll; stop and stare at it.
01:10
BLOBBY — ACTION
Gently lift the lightweight doll and cradle it proudly, turning to face the audience.
01:12
BLOBBY
“BLOBBY! BLOBBY! BLOBBY!”
01:12
🎺 GABRIEL
Play one deliberately awful trumpet note. Everyone freezes, gasps, then recoils dramatically.
01:15
SHEPHERDS 1–5 — ACTION
All five shepherds move together to Blobby’s side, clearly choosing to support him. They smile and give him a little cheer, without getting in Mary’s way.
01:17
JOSEPH
“EXCUSE ME! PUT THE BABY DOWN! THAT’S NOT YOURS!”
01:22

“Joseph, why is a giant pink thing holding our baby?!”
01:27
WISE MAN 1
“I think we've come to the wrong stable.”
01:33
WISE MAN 2
“Can we get the gold back?”
01:37
BLOBBY — ACTION
Gently rock the doll, looking absolutely delighted. The five shepherds stand proudly on Blobby’s side and begin a gentle side-to-side sway together. Joseph tries to approach while Mary holds him back; the five angels gasp silently in exaggerated shock.
01:39
NARRATOR
“Things had taken a very unexpected turn.”
01:43
BLOBBY
“BLOBBY!”
SCENE 401:45–02:20

Blobby chaos

01:45
🎵 SOUND
Start the Mr Blobby song at 0:00 of the recording. Play approximately 23 seconds. Hear song.
01:45
BLOBBY — ACTION
Begin a deliberately silly, gentle dance while holding the doll. All five shepherds sway in time behind Blobby like his backing dancers — left, right, left, right — smiling and copying his movements. Mary and Joseph are unimpressed. The angels react through mime; the wise men shuffle away.
01:52
MARY + BLOBBY
Mary confidently approaches Blobby, holds out her arms and gently takes the doll back. Joseph stays close, looking horrified. The shepherds continue swaying in place and keep the path to the manger clear.
02:00
MARY — ACTION
Return the doll securely to the manger. Blobby now has both hands free.
02:03
JOSEPH
“Right. This is officially the worst night of my life!”
02:08
🎺 GABRIEL + SOUND
Gabriel plays two short notes; sound operator stops the song immediately. Blobby and all five shepherds freeze together, mid-sway.
02:11
NARRATOR
“Finally, peace returned to Bethlehem.”
02:15
BLOBBY
“BLOBBY BLOBBY BLOBBY!”
02:17
MARY
“Right! If you can’t beat him, join him!”
SCENE 502:20–03:50

Everybody dances to Mr Blobby

02:20
🎵 SOUND
Start the 90-second dance excerpt of the same Mr Blobby song, provisionally at 0:40 of the original recording; stop at 2:10. Hear proposed starting point.
02:20–02:36
SHEPHERD 5 + EVERYONE
Shepherd 5 leads the 18 onstage students into two rows of nine; the other four shepherds help everyone form up. The sound and lighting crew stay at their controls. Blobby stands at the front. Gabriel puts his trumpet safely aside and joins the dance.
02:36–03:08
ALL 18 — DANCE
Repeat this 16-count routine: counts 1–4 step right, left, clap twice; 5–8 step right, left, hands up; 9–12 march four times; 13–16 point at Blobby and clap.
03:08–03:24
BLOBBY SOLO
All 18 student performers pause. Blobby does the dance deliberately wrong. The five shepherds keep cheering him on and swaying gently while the others shake their heads.
03:24–03:43
ALL 18 — DANCE
Everyone rejoins the same 16-count routine. Blobby tries and fails to follow.
03:43–03:50
SHEPHERD 5 + GABRIEL
Shepherd 5 leads the cast into a wide semicircle facing Blobby, then the five shepherds kneel on one knee first. All the other performers except Mary, Joseph and Gabriel kneel behind them. Mary and Joseph remain standing at the front, visibly unimpressed. Gabriel steps clear and retrieves the trumpet.
SCENE 603:50–04:00

The final joke

03:50
🎵 SOUND
Stop music instantly. Everyone freezes.
03:51
NARRATOR
“And they all lived happily ever after.”
03:54
🎺 GABRIEL
Play one beautiful, gentle closing note, then lower the trumpet safely.
03:56
BLOBBY
“BLOOOOOOOBBBBBYYYY!” Mary and Joseph exchange a look of total disbelief and begin lowering to one knee. Gabriel can kneel safely once his trumpet is lowered.
03:58
MARY + JOSEPH
Both finish kneeling on one knee, face Blobby and reluctantly shout together: “ALL HAIL BLOBBY!” All other performers remain kneeling silently; Blobby stands triumphant at centre stage.
04:00
LIGHTS / ALL
Lighting operator: BLACKOUT on the end of “BLOBBY!” Everyone holds the one-knee pose; Blobby remains standing. End.
Safety: Gently lift and hand back the lightweight doll; no throwing or rough movement. Shepherds sway in place and leave a clear route for Mary and the baby doll. The trumpet decoration must not interfere with playing. Rehearse the two Mr Blobby music excerpts against the clock before performing. Allow enough room for the one-knee pose; anyone who cannot kneel comfortably may bow instead. Sound and lighting crew stay at their stations.
