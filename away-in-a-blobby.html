Away in a Blobby!

Full rehearsal script — timings, lines and stage actions

4 minutes · 20 students · form tutor as Mr Blobby

Before 00:00: All students are already in Blobby-themed Nativity costumes. The baby doll is in the manger. Gabriel is the trumpet player only (no spoken lines), all five angels are non-speaking and Narrator 2 makes the announcement, the gifts are ready, and the form tutor is hidden at the side entrance. An offstage operator handles the music. All timings are rehearsal targets.

Blobby chaos

SCENE 502:20–03:50

Everybody dances to Mr Blobby

02:20
🎵 SOUND
Start the 90-second dance excerpt of the same Mr Blobby song, provisionally at 0:40 of the original recording; stop at 2:10. Hear proposed starting point.
02:20–02:36
STAR + EVERYONE
Star leads all 20 students into two rows of ten. Blobby stands at the front. Gabriel puts trumpet safely aside and joins the dance.
02:36–03:08
ALL 20 — DANCE
Repeat this 16-count routine: counts 1–4 step right, left, clap twice; 5–8 step right, left, hands up; 9–12 march four times; 13–16 point at Blobby and clap.
03:08–03:24
BLOBBY SOLO
Everyone stops and watches. Blobby does the dance deliberately wrong. Students shake their heads together.
03:24–03:43
ALL 20 — DANCE
Everyone rejoins the same 16-count routine. Blobby tries and fails to follow.
03:43–03:50
STAR + GABRIEL
Star leads everyone into the final Nativity pose. Gabriel moves clear and retrieves trumpet. Everyone holds still.
SCENE 603:50–04:00

The final joke

03:50

04:00
LIGHTS / ALL
Blackout. Hold the pose. End.
Safety: Gently lift and hand back the lightweight doll; no throwing or rough movement. The trumpet decoration must not interfere with playing. Rehearse the two Mr Blobby music excerpts against the clock before performing.
