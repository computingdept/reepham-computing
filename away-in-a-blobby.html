The performance

Complete rehearsal script

Dialogue, stage actions, entrances and music cues — in performance order. Open a scene to rehearse it. All six scenes expand automatically in the printable version.

1 · The Nativity00:00–00:40

Positions: Mary and Joseph ready to walk in. Innkeeper to one side. Narrators visible. Gabriel with trumpet. Angels and shepherds waiting to enter. Blobby hidden.

00:00 🎺 Gabriel: play the first four seconds of the Silent Night trumpet phrase. Trumpet safely lowered afterwards.

00:04NARRATOR 1: Long ago, in Bethlehem, Mary and Joseph were searching for somewhere to stay.

00:11JOSEPH: Have you got a room?

00:14INNKEEPER: Sorry, we're fully booked. There's a stable round the back.

00:19MARY: It'll have to do.

00:20 Mary and Joseph walk to manger. Angels and shepherds enter. Star gestures towards manger.

00:25NARRATOR 2: And that night, something truly extraordinary happened.

00:31GABRIEL: Fear not! I bring wonderful news!

00:36SHEPHERD 1: You could have knocked!

2 · The Wise Men00:40–01:05

00:40 Three wise men enter, each holding their gift. Other shepherds gather. Angels hold the tableau. Star points to manger.

00:44WISE MAN 1: I bring gold.

00:48WISE MAN 2: I bring frankincense.

00:52WISE MAN 3: I bring myrrh.

00:56SHEPHERD 2: Nobody thought to bring nappies?

00:59NARRATOR 1: And there, beneath the star, they discovered the most extraordinary sight...

01:02 🎺 Gabriel: grand fanfare. Group looks toward manger; short silence.

3 · Mr Blobby Appears01:05–01:45

01:05 Blobby stands up safely from behind the cardboard manger frontage — not from inside a box. This is the first time the audience sees the costume.

01:05BLOBBY: BLOBBY! BLOBBY! BLOBBY!

🎺 Gabriel: one intentionally discordant note. All students freeze, then gasp or step back.

01:13JOSEPH: MARY! WHAT IS THAT?!

01:18MARY: DON'T LOOK AT ME!

01:24WISE MAN 1: I think we've come to the wrong stable.

01:30WISE MAN 2: Can we get the gold back?

01:34 Blobby waves and approaches Joseph slowly; Joseph hides behind the shepherds. Angels recoil; Star points at Blobby.

01:37NARRATOR 2: This was not quite how the story was supposed to go.

01:43BLOBBY: BLOBBY!

4 · Blobby Chaos01:45–02:20

01:45 🎵 Sound: play the opening 0:00–0:23 of Mr Blobby (1993). This is the comedy solo clip, not the later group-dance excerpt.

Blobby dances terribly. Angels recoil dramatically, shepherds attempt to restore order, wise men shuffle backwards. Nothing involves contact or a fall.

02:03JOSEPH: Somebody do something!

02:08 🎺 Gabriel plays two loud notes; Sound cuts the music instantly; Blobby freezes.

02:11NARRATOR 1: Finally, peace returned to Bethlehem.

02:15BLOBBY: BLOBBY BLOBBY BLOBBY!

02:17MARY: Oh, just join in!

5 · The Christmas Dance02:20–03:50

02:20 🎵 Sound: restart Mr Blobby from approximately 0:40 in the original video; stop at about 2:10 of the recording (90 seconds). No dialogue over this section.

02:20–02:36 Star leads everyone into two rows of ten. Blobby takes centre front. Gabriel places trumpet down safely before joining the dance.

02:36–03:08 · ALL 20: group choreography.

Repeat the 16-count sequence: 1–4 step right, left, clap twice. 5–8 step right, left, hands up. 9–12 march four counts. 13–16 point at Blobby and clap.

03:08–03:24 · BLOBBY SOLO.

Everyone freezes and watches as Blobby gets the moves wrong. Students shake their heads in unison.

03:24–03:43 · ALL 20: dance together again.

03:43–03:50 Star calls everyone into final Nativity tableau. Gabriel steps out carefully to retrieve trumpet; everyone else holds the pose.

6 · The Final Joke03:50–04:00

03:50 🎵 Sound: stop music dead. Cast freezes.

03:51NARRATOR 1: And they all lived happily ever after.

03:54 🎺 Gabriel: one lovely, gentle closing trumpet note.

03:56BLOBBY: BLOOOOOOOBBBBBYYYY!

03:58 EVERYONE: NOOOOO!

04:00 BLACKOUT / END. Hold the final freeze until lights go out.

Music, props & practical jobs

🎵 Mr Blobby soundtrack and trumpet

Gabriel: Silent Night opening phrase (00:00), dramatic fanfare (01:02), wrong note (01:05), freeze signal (02:08), beautiful closing note (03:54).

Sound operator: play Mr Blobby (1993) recording 0:00–0:23 at performance 01:45–02:08; stop for the freeze; then play approximately 0:40–2:10 of the same song at performance 02:20–03:50. Cut at exactly 03:50.

The two dance and comedy excerpts come from the same 1993 Mr Blobby single. The preview below is an external YouTube video; for the show, cue the music with the event's playback setup. Verify the excerpt boundaries against the recording you use.

Assign a person who is not performing to operate the PA, or arrange playback with the event technician.

🎭 Costumes and props

Manger frontage or screen to conceal Blobby, blanket, three gifts, shepherd staffs, simple halos, Bethlehem star, traditional Nativity costumes and Mr Blobby costume.

Safety: Blobby must have room to enter safely and clear sightlines; no falls or collisions. Keep the trumpet in a safe place while dancing.

We are parodying a school performance going wrong — the joke is the surprise character and the chaos.
