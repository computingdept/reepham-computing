=== SLIDE 8 ===
Silent Working – 10 Minutes
CPU: Central Processing Unit, the computer’s brain.
ALU: Arithmetic Logic Unit, does maths and logic.
CU: Control Unit, tells parts of the CPU what to do.
Cache: Small, fast memory inside the CPU.
Clock Speed: How many instructions the CPU runs each second.
