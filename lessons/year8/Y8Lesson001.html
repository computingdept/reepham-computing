I DO · EXPECTATIONS

Three things matter

Respect others. Respect the room. Respect learning.

Respect others

SLANT when the teacher is speaking. Listen when others contribute. Support the learning of others.

Respect the classroom

Leave desks as you found them. Keep food and drink away from desks. Follow the Acceptable Use Agreement.

Respect learning

Be ready to answer questions and complete tasks. Computing work is usually presented clearly and carefully. Keep working when something is difficult.

SLANT

SSit upSit straight, face forward, ready to learn.

YOU DO · ORGANISATION

Get your Computing folder ready

Work through these in order.

Presentation of work · Green folder
  • Complete the cover page at the front.
  • Keep pages in chronological order, with new work at the back.
Presentation of work · Do Nows and assessments
  • Write in black or blue ink.
  • Mark in coloured pen.
  • Use your best handwriting.
  • Cross out mistakes with one line.
  • Keep pages neat and use full sentences.
Presentation of work · Paper and Class Notebook
  • Paper: add title and date; underline headings; label diagrams.
  • Class Notebook: add the date, use black font, mark in purple, organise with headings/lists, and crop screenshots so they are not distorted.

I DO · SAFE & RESPONSIBLE USE

Filtering and monitoring

Filtering

If the school thinks a website is inappropriate, it may be blocked.

If you think a website should or should not be blocked, tell a teacher.

Monitoring

Staff can see activity on school systems, including device screens, internet history, files and emails.

School systems should be used for schoolwork.

Acceptable Use Agreement: the source lesson states that breaking the agreement can result in a negative on Arbor, and two negatives trigger a lunchtime detention.
Password reminder: the lesson states that passwords must have 10 characters and should not need changing all year.
