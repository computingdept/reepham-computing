Three things matter

Respect for others

SLANT when the teacher is speaking. Listen when others contribute. Support the learning of others.

Respect for the classroom

Leave desks as you found them. Keep food and drink away from desks. Follow the Acceptable Use Agreement.

Respect for learning

Be ready to answer questions and complete tasks. Computing work should usually be presented clearly and carefully. Work hard even when something is difficult.

SLANT

S Sit up · L Listen carefully · A Ask and answer · N Never interrupt · T Track the speaker

Get your Computing folder ready

Green folder presentation
  • Complete the cover page at the front.
  • Keep pages in chronological order, with new work at the back.
Do Nows and assessments
  • Write in black or blue ink and mark in coloured pen.
  • Use your best handwriting and cross out mistakes with one line.
  • Keep pages neat and write in full sentences.
Paper work and Class Notebook
  • Paper: add title/date, underline headings with a ruler, label diagrams.
  • Class Notebook: add dates, use black font and purple marking, organise with headings/lists, crop screenshots neatly.
I DoListeningCold Calling

Filtering and monitoring

Filtering

If the school thinks a website is inappropriate, it may be blocked. If you think a site should or should not be blocked, tell a teacher.

Monitoring

Staff can see activity on school systems, including screens, internet history, files and emails. School systems should be used for schoolwork.

Acceptable Use: the original lesson states that breaking the agreement can result in a negative on Arbor, and two negatives trigger a lunchtime detention.
Password reminder: the lesson states that passwords must have 10 characters and should not need changing all year.
