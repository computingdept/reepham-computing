Computing Expectations

1 · Respect for Others

SLANT when the teacher is speaking.
Listen when others contribute.
Support the learning of others.

2 · Respect for the Classroom Environment

Leave desks as you found them.
Keep food and drink away from desks.
Follow the Acceptable Use Agreement.

3 · Respect for Learning

Be ready to answer questions and complete tasks.
Folders and Class Notebooks should be well presented.
Work hard even when you find it difficult.

Presentation of Work

Green Folders

  • Completed cover page at the front.
  • Pages in chronological order.

Do Nows & Assessments

  • Black or blue ink.
  • Mark in coloured pen.
  • Best handwriting.
  • Mistakes crossed out with one line.
  • No scribbles, doodles or graffiti.
  • Write in full sentences.

Paper Based Lessons

  • Title and date at the top.
  • Underline titles and headings with a ruler.
  • Diagrams/charts neatly drawn and labelled.

Class Notebook

  • Date each row.
  • Black font; purple marking.
  • Titles/headings bold or underlined.
  • Use bullet points/numbering.
  • Crop screenshots and images.

What is filtering?

If you search and try to go on a website that the school thinks is inappropriate, it will not allow you to access it.

If you think a website should/should not be blocked please let a teacher know.

What is monitoring?

Staff can see your school device screen, internet history, files and emails at any time.

New This Year

  • If you break the Acceptable Use Agreement you will receive a negative on Arbor.
  • Two negatives will trigger a Lunchtime Detention.
