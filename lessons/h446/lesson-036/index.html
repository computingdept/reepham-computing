QUESTION 1 OF 4 · LAST LESSON · L035 · REVISION

Retrieval

Why should a student show the steps of a binary calculation rather than writing only the final result?

MODEL ANSWER

Clear working makes the method verifiable and can earn method marks even if the final result is wrong.

QUESTION 2 OF 4 · EARLIER · L016 · ARRAYS

Retrieval

What is the difference between an array index and the value stored at that index?

MODEL ANSWER

The index identifies a position; the value is the item stored in that position.

QUESTION 3 OF 4 · EARLIER · L020 · STACKS

Retrieval

What does LIFO mean when removing an item from a stack?

MODEL ANSWER

Last In, First Out: the most recently added item is removed first.

QUESTION 4 OF 4 · CHALLENGE · L024 · SEARCHING

Challenge

Why might an ordered collection allow a faster search method than an unordered collection?

MODEL ANSWER

Ordering lets a search discard portions of the collection rather than inspecting every item.
