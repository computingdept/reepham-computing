Part 1 · Self-review your map

Self-check criteria for a student-created A3 knowledge map. These are not model answers to an assessment. Look at your own explanation before checking the guide.

Data representation

  • Number bases and correct conversion method
  • Signed representation including two’s complement
  • Mantissa, exponent and normalisation
  • Floating-point operations and bitwise ideas

Data structures

  • Arrays/records/lists/tuples distinguished
  • Stack LIFO and queue FIFO operations
  • Tree/BST with links or pointers
  • Linked lists, graphs and hashes included

Learning evidence

  • At least one original worked example
  • Definition, method, misconception and link used
  • At least two correct cross-links
  • Green-pen improvements made after retrieval

Green-pen routine: Put a tick beside accurate explanations, improve one misconception, and star the next topic to revisit. Keep your original attempt visible.
