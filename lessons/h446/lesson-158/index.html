I DO · MODELLED APPLICATION

A pupil correctly guesses a teacher's password, then signs in to view private records despite knowing permission is absent. Which offence may apply?

STEP 1 OF 3

Password discovered

Identify the relevant facts, purpose and authority.

STEP 2 OF 3

Login not authorised

Discuss which law or safeguard fits the situation.

STEP 3 OF 3

Section 1 risk

Section 1 can apply to knowing unauthorised access even if no data is changed. The correct password does not supply authorisation. Additional offences depend on intent and conduct; do not assume every access is a section 2 offence.

WE DO · DIFFERENT CASE

An employee, without authority, deliberately launches an action intended to disable a school server. Which issue arises?

STEP 1 OF 3

Action without authority

Identify the relevant facts, purpose and authority.

STEP 2 OF 3

Service operation impaired

Discuss which law or safeguard fits the situation.

STEP 3 OF 3

Section 3 issue

Section 3 concerns unauthorised acts intended to impair computer operation, or reckless impairment. Evidence of deliberate disruption distinguishes this from merely accessing a file. Serious-damage provisions may apply to particularly grave cases.
