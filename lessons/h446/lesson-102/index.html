Lesson 102 · Choosing RISC, CISC, GPU and Multicore Systems

By the end you can…
  • RISC/CISC and CPU/GPU are not a single four-way classification: a processor's ISA, core organisation and accelerators are different design properties.
  • A graphics or machine-learning workload with abundant parallel operations may benefit from a GPU; sequential/control-intensive jobs suit general CPU cores.
  • Choose based on power, energy budget, latency, software compatibility, transfer overhead and ability to divide the workload; support claims with mechanism.

I DO · MODEL

Recommend hardware for battery-powered sensor making a small control decision each second.

WORKED REASONING

Explain the choice

A low-power embedded CPU (often using a RISC architecture) is reasonable; no large GPU is needed for tiny sequential work.

WE DO · DIFFERENT EXAMPLE

A studio renders millions of independent image elements per frame. Recommend computing hardware.

WORKED REASONING

Explain the choice

A GPU can accelerate similar independent pixel/shading operations, paired with a CPU for control.
