QUESTION 3 OF 4

Use two’s complement

Represent −26 as an eight-bit two’s-complement integer. Show your method.

ANSWER
+26 = 00011010; invert → 11100101; add 1 → 11100110.
QUESTION 4 OF 4

Explain a fixed-width shift

Logically shift 10110000 one place left in an unsigned byte. Give the stored result and explain why it is not double the original value.

ANSWER
01100000 = 96. The leading 1 is discarded; 176 × 2 = 352 exceeds the byte’s maximum 255.

FLOATING-POINT REFERENCE

Fraction · mantissa · exponent

Binary fractions:
½  ¼  ⅛  ¹⁄₁₆ ...

8-bit mantissa:
−1 | ½ ¼ ⅛ ¹⁄₁₆ ¹⁄₃₂ ¹⁄₆₄ ¹⁄₁₂₈

4-bit exponent:
−8 4 2 1
Value = mantissa × 2ᴱ. The mantissa’s binary point is immediately after the sign bit.
STEP 1 OF 4

Binary fractions continue right of the point

6.5 = 4 + 2 + ½
    = 110.1₂

0.375 = ¼ + ⅛
      = 0.011₂
1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Floating point uses two signed fields

The mantissa stores the signed significant fraction. The exponent stores the signed power-of-two scale.

There is no separate sign bit outside these fields and no exponent bias in this format.
2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Normalise the mantissa

For a non-zero value, a positive normalised mantissa begins 01; a negative one begins 10.

If redundant 00 or 11 sign bits remain, shift the mantissa left and decrease the exponent by the same number of places.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Precision and range

More mantissa bits improve precision. More exponent bits increase range.

Zero is a special case: use all-zero fields rather than trying to normalise it.
4 of 4
ONE COMPLETE WORKED EXAMPLE

Encode +6.5 in 8+4 floating point

6.5₁₀ → ?

Use an eight-bit mantissa and four-bit exponent.

Worked example
STEP 1 · DENARY → BINARY

Write 6.5 in binary

6.5 = 110.1₂

The whole-number part is 110; the 0.5 fractional part is .1.

STEP 2 · NORMALISE

Move the binary point

110.1₂
= 0.1101₂ × 2³

The binary point moves three places left, so the exponent is 3.

STEP 3 · ENCODE & CHECK

Write the two fields

Mantissa: 01101000
Exponent: 0011

Stored: 01101000 0011

Check: 0.8125 × 8 = 6.5.

Final representation = 01101000 0011
