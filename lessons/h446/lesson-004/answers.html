Working and marking guidance

The mantissa was doubled but the scale stayed the same, so the value doubled (1). Reduce exponent 3 to 2 / 0010 (1). Correct fields: 01010000 0010 (1). Normalisation removes redundant sign bits to make full use of the available significant bits (1).
