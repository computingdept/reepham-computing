I DO · TEACHER MODEL

A bank is building a novel payment verification system with security uncertainty. Justify the spiral model.

WORKED REASONING

Model answer

Each cycle sets goals, identifies risks such as fraud or performance, prototypes/assesses the riskiest elements, evaluates with stakeholders then plans the next cycle. Risk reduction drives the project.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

A museum app needs a basic pilot within weeks but must support disabled visitors. Select a method.

STEP 1 · IDENTIFY

What must we decide?

Identify the inputs, relevant facts and exact output or judgement required.

STEP 2 · APPLY

Work through the method

Use the middle stage of the visual to follow the process and state each intermediate result.

STEP 3 · EXPLAIN

Shared worked answer

RAD/agile could produce accessible prototypes quickly with visitor feedback, but test accessibility systematically. Spiral may be excessive for a small low-risk app; compare project scale and uncertainty.
