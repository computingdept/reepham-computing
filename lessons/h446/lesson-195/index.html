Read and interpret

A  = 0.1000₂ × 2³ = 4
B  = 0.1000₂ × 2² = 2
Align B: 0.0100₂ × 2³
Sum:     0.1100₂ × 2³ = 6
STEP 1 OF 3

Align exponents

Before adding mantissas, both terms need the same power of two. Shift B right one place when raising its exponent by one.

STEP 2 OF 3

Add the mantissas

0.1000₂ + 0.0100₂ = 0.1100₂; keep the common exponent 3.

STEP 3 OF 3

Check in decimal

0.1100₂ × 2³ = 0.75 × 8 = 6, matching 4 + 2.

Foundation · 1 OF 3

Why must exponents be aligned before binary floating-point addition?

MODEL ANSWER

So corresponding mantissa bits represent the same place values.

Core · 2 OF 3

Calculate and normalise 0.1010 × 2³ − 0.1000 × 2². Retain all shown mantissa bits during working.

MODEL ANSWER

Align B to 0.0100 × 2³. Subtract: 0.1010 − 0.0100 = 0.0110 × 2³. Shift left and decrease exponent: 0.1100 × 2² = 3. No shown bits are discarded.

Stretch · 3 OF 3

Explain how exponent alignment can lose precision when the mantissa has a fixed number of bits.

MODEL ANSWER

Right-shifting may discard significant low-order bits. They cannot all be held in the available mantissa, so rounding or truncation can produce an inexact result.
