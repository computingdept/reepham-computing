Network types, architecture and hardware

By the end you can…
  • A LAN covers a local area such as a school building; a WAN connects networks across wider geography.
  • Client-server uses dedicated service systems; peer-to-peer allows peers to exchange roles and resources, with different management implications.
  • NICs connect devices, switches forward local Ethernet frames, wireless APs bridge Wi-Fi, and routers forward IP packets between networks.

EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 6 MARKS · 6 MINUTES

A charity installs a client–server network in a community art centre. Name THREE distinct items of networking hardware, other than the computers and servers, and describe the function of each. [6]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q6(d) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 6 MARKS

Suitable examples: switch connects devices and forwards frames to recipients; router joins networks and routes data packets; wireless access point allows Wi-Fi clients; NIC connects a device to a network and has a MAC address; Ethernet cable carries signals; bridge joins segments; firewall filters traffic; repeater regenerates a signal.

Marking: Three hardware-plus-function pairs at two marks each. A name alone earns one of the pair, development of its actual role earns the other. Total six.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

A sports centre connects its office, reception and finance computers to one local area network. State three different benefits of this arrangement. [3]

OCR H446/01 · June 2023 · Q1(d)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Examples: share printers; share permitted files; centralised access permissions/security; common internet connection; central backups; easier support and software updates.

Marking: One mark for each different, practical LAN benefit, maximum three.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 4 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

A customer's phone and the bookshop web server use standard protocols such as TCP/IP and HTTP. Explain what a network protocol is and why agreed standards help their different devices communicate. [2]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q6(c) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

A protocol is a collection of agreed rules governing network data transfer. Shared protocols make communication predictable and interoperable between systems using different hardware/operating systems.

Marking: One mark for rules (plural) of communication, one for standardisation/interoperability/predictable data format.

RHS ADDITIONAL PRACTICE · QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

A small design company is deciding between a peer-to-peer network and a client-server network. Explain two advantages of using a client-server network for its confidential project files. [4]

RHS original follow-on question · linked to this lesson's exam practice

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Access permissions can be managed centrally, helping prevent unauthorised access to files. Files can be backed up centrally, reducing the risk of losing project data.

Marking: Two marks per distinct advantage: one for the feature and one for explaining its benefit to the company.
