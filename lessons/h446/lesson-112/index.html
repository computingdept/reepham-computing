QUESTION 1 OF 4

What role does a device driver perform?

MODEL ANSWER

It lets the OS communicate with/control a specific hardware device.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

A linked-list node has data and what other essential field?

MODEL ANSWER

A link/reference to the next node (for a singly linked list).

QUESTION 3 OF 4

In a BST, where is a value less than the current node placed?

MODEL ANSWER

In its left subtree (under the chosen no-duplicate convention).

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Why can an array provide direct indexed access?

MODEL ANSWER

Its element locations can be calculated from base address and index.

I DO · TEACHER MODEL

A clinic manages thousands of appointments accessed concurrently. Spreadsheet or database application?

WORKED REASONING

Model answer

A DBMS is better: structured records, querying, concurrency, constraints and access rights. A spreadsheet is useful for small-scale analysis but is not ideal as a multi-user transactional store.
