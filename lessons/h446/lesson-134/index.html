I DO · MODELLED

A pupil books several clubs. Why store pupils and club bookings in separate tables?

STEP 1 OF 3

Pupil ID 7: Sam

Find the given data and name the required result.

STEP 2 OF 3

Bookings refer to PupilID 7

Explain why this intermediate stage is necessary.

STEP 3 OF 3

Change name once

Pupil(PupilID PK, Name) and Booking(BookingID PK, PupilID FK, ClubID FK) separate data. A pupil name is held once and each booking refers to the pupil by ID, reducing update anomalies.

WE DO · DIFFERENT CASE

A school has Device(DeviceID, Model) and Loan(LoanID, DeviceID, Borrower). Identify suitable keys.

STEP 1 OF 3

Device PK DeviceID

Find the given data and name the required result.

STEP 2 OF 3

Loan PK LoanID

Explain why this intermediate stage is necessary.

STEP 3 OF 3

Loan FK DeviceID

DeviceID uniquely identifies each device (PK). LoanID identifies each loan (PK). Loan.DeviceID is an FK to Device.DeviceID. Borrower may later reference a Borrower table.

Data kept in one simple table or file without relational links.

True or false?

A foreign key value must always be unique in its table.

Model answer

False. Many child records may share one valid parent key.
