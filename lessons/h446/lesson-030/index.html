CONSOLIDATE

Three related circuit ideas

D-type flip-flop → stores 1 bit
Half adder       → adds 2 bits
Full adder       → adds 2 bits + carry-in
Today is about connecting the ideas rather than introducing new content.
STEP 1 OF 3

D-type flip-flop

At clock trigger:
Q ← D

Between triggers:
Q holds

The output represents stored state.
