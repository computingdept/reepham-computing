QUESTION 1 OF 4

Last lesson

What row and column does move 8 map to?

ANSWER

row 2, column 1.

(8 - 1) / 3 = 2 and (8 - 1) % 3 = 1.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

Last lesson

ANSWER

What is the difference between a function and a procedure?

ANSWER

How many winning lines are possible?

ANSWER
