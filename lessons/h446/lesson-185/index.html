QUESTION 1 OF 4

Pass

What is one pass in bubble sort?

ANSWER

One complete movement through the unsorted part, comparing adjacent pairs.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

No swaps

What does a no-swap pass tell you?

ANSWER

The list is sorted.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Index

What is the first valid index of a typical zero-indexed array?

ANSWER

0.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Single item

Why is a single-item list already sorted?

ANSWER

There are no other items that could be out of order relative to it.

Key vocabulary for today
insertion sortsorted sectionunsorted sectioncurrent itempositioninsertshiftindex
TERM 1 OF 8

insertion sort

DEFINITION

A sorting algorithm that takes each next item and inserts it into the correct position in the sorted part.

TERM 2 OF 8

sorted section

DEFINITION

The part of the data already in order.

TERM 3 OF 8

unsorted section

DEFINITION

The items not yet inserted into their final relative order.

TERM 4 OF 8

current item

DEFINITION

The next value being inserted into the sorted section.

TERM 5 OF 8

position

DEFINITION

The index currently being checked while placing the item.

TERM 6 OF 8

insert

DEFINITION

Place an item into the correct position within an ordered sequence.

TERM 7 OF 8

shift

DEFINITION

Move an existing item to make room for the item being inserted.

TERM 8 OF 8

index

DEFINITION

The numbered position of an item in an array.

STANDARD ALGORITHM · SORTING

Take next item → insert into sorted section

Start: 8  3  6  2

Sorted section begins:
[8] 3 6 2

Insert 3:
[3 8] 6 2

Insert 6:
[3 6 8] 2
Key idea: Insertion sort grows a sorted section from the left. The next item is moved into the correct place within that section.
STEP 1 OF 4

Treat the first item as sorted

[8]  3  6  2

A one-item sequence is already in order.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Take the next item

current item = 3

Compare it with items in the sorted section.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Move it into the correct place

[3 8]  6  2

The sorted section has grown by one.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Repeat

[2 3 6 8]

Continue until every item has been inserted.

4 of 4
WORKED EXAMPLE

Sort 9, 4, 7, 3 ascending

9  4  7  3
Model the method clearly
STEP 1

Insert 4

[9] 4 7 3
→ [4 9] 7 3

4 belongs before 9.

STEP 2

Insert 7

[4 9] 7 3
→ [4 7 9] 3

7 goes between 4 and 9.

STEP 3

Insert 3

[4 7 9] 3
→ [3 4 7 9]

3 moves to the front of the sorted section.

STEP 4

Final check

3 4 7 9

Every item now belongs to the sorted section.
