Data capture, integrity and ACID transactions

By the end you can…
  • Data capture gathers data, while validation, verification and referential integrity address different correctness concerns.
  • Atomicity ensures a transaction is all-or-nothing; consistency preserves constraints; isolation addresses concurrent interference; durability keeps committed results.
  • Row locking, transactions and appropriate constraints help prevent two concurrent changes from corrupting financial or stock records.

EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

A museum processes an online membership purchase. Describe the ACID requirement of durability for the completed transaction. [2]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q2(d)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

After COMMIT the data must not be lost, even if power or a server fails.

Marking: One for committed data persisting; one for failure protection.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 1 MARK · 1 MINUTE

State one implementation technique supporting transaction durability. [1]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q2(d)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 1 MARKS

Store committed information on nonvolatile secondary storage, with appropriate durable logging/flush.

Marking: One for persistence beyond volatile RAM.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

Two sales agents try to update one booking simultaneously. Explain how RECORD LOCKING helps the isolation of transactions. [3]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q2(d)(iii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Concurrent operations should behave as if serial. A record lock restricts conflicting access while a transaction changes a record, preventing lost updates or reads of uncommitted data.

Marking: One mark for isolation outcome, one for record exclusive access and one for preventing interleaved conflicting writes/reads.

EXAM QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 1 MARK · 1 MINUTE

Give one disadvantage of locking database records during busy periods. [1]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q2(d)(iv) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 1 MARKS

Delays while another transaction holds a lock, or deadlock.

Marking: One mark for either.
