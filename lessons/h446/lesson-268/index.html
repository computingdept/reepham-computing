Five process scheduling algorithms

By the end you can…
  • FCFS serves arrival order; SJF selects the shortest available complete job.
  • SRT can preempt when a newly ready job has shorter remaining time; RR cycles by quantum.
  • Multilevel feedback queues adapt priority by task behaviour. Compare responsiveness, waits, starvation and context-switch overhead.
