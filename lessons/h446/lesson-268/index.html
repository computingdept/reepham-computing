EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 5 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

Describe three features of how this scheduling method manages processes. [3]

GIVEN INFORMATION

A desktop operating system uses a multi-level feedback queue to schedule its active processes.

OCR H446/01 · June 2025 · Q1(d)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Processes occupy different priority queues. Different queues may use different time slices or scheduling rules. Processes can change queues according to their behaviour or priority; the scheduler balances responsive and longer-running work.

Marking: One mark for each distinct accurate scheduling feature, maximum three. Avoid granting several marks for repeated descriptions of priority.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 5 · 5 MARKS · 5 MINUTES

A school server uses several process schedulers: Round Robin (RR), First Come First Served (FCFS), Shortest Job First (SJF), Shortest Remaining Time (SRT) and Multi-level Feedback Queue (MLFQ). Match each numbered description with the correct abbreviated scheduler. 1. The job with least remaining work is chosen and a running job can be interrupted by a newer shorter job. 2. Each ready job receives a repeated fixed-length CPU time slice. 3. Jobs can be placed in and move between priority queues according to behaviour. 4. Jobs run in order of arrival. 5. The waiting job with smallest total expected duration runs until completion. [5]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q2(d)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 5 MARKS

1 SRT; 2 RR; 3 MLFQ; 4 FCFS; 5 SJF.

Marking: One mark for each correct matching, maximum five. Each scheduler must match the specific description.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 5 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

Why is FIRST COME FIRST SERVED suitable for a shared printer where all submitted jobs have equal priority? [2]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q6(a)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

Each job has equal priority; the printer takes jobs in arrival order, a FIFO queue.

Marking: One for equal priority, one for print in arrival order.

EXAM QUESTION 4 OF 5 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

Explain how ROUND ROBIN scheduling keeps a browser, ticket app and editor responsive on one processor. [3]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q6(a)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Each ready process receives a time quantum; unfinished work returns to the ready queue; the scheduler cycles between them to make progress on multiple jobs.

Marking: One for time slicing, one for repeated cycling/requeue, one for multitasking responsiveness.

EXAM QUESTION 5 OF 5 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

Give a CPU scheduling method other than FCFS or Round Robin, and explain its selection rule. [2]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q6(a)(iii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

Shortest Job First chooses the job with shortest expected run duration; Shortest Remaining Time favours shortest remaining job with preemption; multi-level feedback schedules across changing-priority queues.

Marking: One for valid different method, one for characteristic operation.
