Lesson 067 · Divide and Conquer: Splitting the Search

By the end you can…
  • Divide and conquer breaks a problem into smaller instances, solves them, and (where relevant) combines results.
  • Binary search repeatedly discards half of a SORTED array. It is an example of reducing to one subproblem rather than solving both.
  • Merge sort divides into halves and combines them in sorted order. Dividing alone is not enough; correctness depends on valid preconditions and combining.
