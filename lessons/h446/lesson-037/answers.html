Part 2 · Self-review your completed map

Self-check criteria for a student-created A3 knowledge map. These are not model answers to an assessment. Look at your own explanation before checking the guide.

Boolean logic

  • Basic Boolean gates and truth tables
  • Simplification and valid K-map groupings
  • Half/full adders and D-type storage explained

Programming

  • Sequence, selection and iteration
  • Local/global scope and modularity
  • Procedures/functions and by-value/by-reference decisions

Algorithms

  • Linear search steps
  • Binary search steps and ordered-data precondition
  • Comparison and a justified contextual choice

Connections and next steps

  • A meaningful link between representation and logic
  • A link between structures and code
  • A link between data ordering and search choice
  • At least one personal revision priority

Green-pen routine: Put a tick beside accurate explanations, improve one misconception, and star the next topic to revisit. Keep your original attempt visible.
