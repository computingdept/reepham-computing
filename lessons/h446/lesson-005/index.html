QUESTION 1 OF 4

Represent 6.5 in normalised floating-point form using an eight-bit two’s-complement mantissa and a four-bit two’s-complement exponent.

ANSWER

01101000 0011

6.5 = 110.1₂ = 0.1101₂ × 2³. Mantissa 01101000; exponent 0011.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

2–3 lessons back

Convert 6D₁₆ to eight-bit binary and denary.

ANSWER

01101101₂ and 109.

6 → 0110, D → 1101. In denary: (6 × 16) + 13 = 109.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Earlier retrieval

Decode 11110110 as an 8-bit two’s-complement integer.

ANSWER

−10

−128 + 64 + 32 + 16 + 4 + 2 = −10.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Challenge

Apply AND 00001111 to 10110110. Give the result in binary and hexadecimal.

ANSWER

00000110₂ = 06₁₆

AND with 00001111 clears the upper nibble and preserves the lower nibble 0110.

TERM 6 OF 6

Two’s complement

DEFINITION

In this lesson, both the mantissa and exponent are interpreted using two’s complement.

Split → evaluate → scale

8-bit mantissa: −1 | ½  ¼  ⅛  ¹⁄₁₆  ¹⁄₃₂  ¹⁄₆₄  ¹⁄₁₂₈
4-bit exponent: −8   4   2   1

Value = mantissa × 2ᴱ
Two signs, two jobs:
the mantissa controls the sign of the value; the exponent controls its scale.
STEP 1 OF 5

Split the fields

Separate the bit pattern into the mantissa and the exponent at the stated field boundary.

Treat the two fields separately before doing any calculation.
1 of 5
STEP 2 OF 5

Evaluate the mantissa

Add the place values for the bits that are 1.

Mantissa places:
−1 | ½  ¼  ⅛  ¹⁄₁₆  ¹⁄₃₂  ¹⁄₆₄  ¹⁄₁₂₈
If the sign bit is 1, include the −1 place value.
2 of 5
STEP 3 OF 5

Convert the exponent to denary

Read the exponent as a signed two’s-complement integer.

4-bit exponent places:
−8   4   2   1
A negative exponent changes the scale. It does not make the value negative.
3 of 5
STEP 4 OF 5

Apply the scale

Value = mantissa × 2ᴱ

A positive exponent multiplies by a power of two. A negative exponent divides by a power of two.

Example: 2⁻² = ¼.
4 of 5
STEP 5 OF 5

Check the representation

For a non-zero normalised mantissa:

  • 01 starts a positive normalised mantissa.
  • 10 starts a negative normalised mantissa.
More mantissa bits improve precision. More exponent bits increase range.

STEP 2 · EXPONENT

= 0.6875 × 8
= 5.5
