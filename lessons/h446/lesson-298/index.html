NEW LEARNING · WORKED EXAMPLE FIRST

Trace and predict

bool Search(int[] a,int i,int total,int target){
 if(total==target) return true;
 if(i==a.Length || total>target) return false;
 return Search(a,i+1,total+a[i],target)
     || Search(a,i+1,total,target);
}
// Non-negative a only; choose/exclude each element.
STEP 1 OF 3

Make a decision

At each position choose the current item or skip it. Track index and running total.

STEP 2 OF 3

Reject a dead end

When the sum exceeds the target (non-negative inputs) or the list ends, undo the choice.

STEP 3 OF 3

Backtrack

Try the other branch; success means a valid selection has been found. Exponential worst-case growth remains possible.

I DO · WORKED MODEL

Find a subset totalling 6 from [2,3,4]. Trace the first failed path and successful branch.

Include 2, include 3 → 5
Include 4 → 9 reject
Undo 4; exclude 4 → dead end at 5
Backtrack: exclude 3; include 4 → 6 success
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

The first path over-shoots: 2+3+4=9, so reject that branch.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the method

Undo 4, skip 3, then include 4 → 6.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

Find a subset totalling 5 from [1,3,4] using inclusion/exclusion.

Include 1 and 3 →4
Include 4 →8 reject
Undo 4; exclude 4 → dead end at 4
Backtrack: skip 3; include 4 →5 success
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Including 1 and 3 produces 4; adding 4 overshoots 5.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the method

Undo 4, skip 3, then include 4 → 5.
