1.4.2(b)(c) · LINKED LIST

Data + link

Head
 ↓

You need both the behaviour of the structure and the algorithms used to create, traverse, add and remove data.
STEP 1 OF 4

Head starts the list

Node head = new Node(10);

The program needs a reference to the first node.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Add links nodes

head.Next = new Node(20);
head.Next.Next = new Node(30);

Each Next reference extends the chain.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Traverse with a temporary reference

Node current = head;
while (current != null)
{
    Console.WriteLine(current.Data);
    current = current.Next;
}

Move current along the list without changing Head.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Remove by bypassing

head.Next = head.Next.Next;

For 10 → 20 → 30, this changes the logical list to 10 → 30.

4 of 4
WORKED EXAMPLE

Traverse 10 → 20 → 30

Follow each Next reference until null.

Head → 10 → 20 → 30 → null
