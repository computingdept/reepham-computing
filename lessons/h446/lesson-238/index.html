NEW LEARNING · CODE FIRST

Follow the example

// Library loan model
record Book(string ISBN, bool Available);
// Keep attributes needed to decide a loan.
// Omit cover colour and physical weight unless needed.
STEP 1 OF 3

Choose a purpose

Decide whether a particular book may be loaned.

STEP 2 OF 3

Keep essential facts

ISBN identifies the book, and Available indicates whether it can be borrowed.

STEP 3 OF 3

Evaluate omissions

The model cannot answer questions about book weight or appearance because those attributes are omitted.

I DO · TEACHER MODEL

Model a bus network for finding the route with the fewest stops.

Nodes = stops; edges = permitted connections.
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Model each bus stop as a vertex and each permitted connection as an edge.

STEP 2 OF 2

Model and check

Vehicle colour is irrelevant. To find fastest rather than fewest-stop routes, add journey-time weights.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

Model the average of a class's valid test marks.

average = sum(validMarks) / count(validMarks)
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Only valid marks and the number of entries are needed to compute the mean.

STEP 2 OF 2

Model and check

Define how to handle missing results. Other pupil attributes need not be stored for this calculation.

Core · 6 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

Give a simple abstraction for detecting school room timetable clashes: three needed fields and two omitted details.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Room identifier [1], lesson start [1], lesson end [1]; compare overlapping times in same room [1]; omit poster colour [1] and desk appearance [1].

Stretch · 6 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

Why should a model not contain every real-world detail? Give an example where a previously omitted detail becomes relevant.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Reduces storage [1], complexity [1] and irrelevant data exposure [1]; keeps purpose clear [1]; journey duration becomes relevant when switching from fewest stops to fastest route [2].

TERM 1 OF 5

abstraction

Retain relevant characteristics, ignore irrelevant ones.

TERM 2 OF 5

model

Simplified representation serving a purpose.

TERM 3 OF 5

attribute

Property stored about an entity.

TERM 4 OF 5

assumption

Condition accepted in constructing the model.

TERM 5 OF 5

limitation

A question the simplified model cannot answer.

True or false?

A good abstraction must record every real-world property.

Model answer

False. It records properties relevant to the model's purpose.
