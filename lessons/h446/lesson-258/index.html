Key vocabulary

NEW LEARNING · EXAMPLE FIRST

Trace the concept

PC=72 → MAR=72
Memory[72] → MDR → CIR
Sequential PC advances to 73.
Multiple cores help only independent work.
STEP 1 OF 3

Fetch the instruction

PC identifies address 72, which MAR sends to memory. MDR transfers its contents to CIR.

STEP 2 OF 3

Differentiate performance improvements

Cores can execute independent work; cache helps when needed data/instructions are present.

STEP 3 OF 3

Explain limits

Branching, shared memory bandwidth, serial dependencies and software constrain real speedup.

I DO · MODEL

Starting PC=40, trace MAR, CIR and PC after a sequential fetch.

PC=40 → MAR=40
CIR=Memory[40]
PC=41
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

Copy PC=40 into MAR to select the instruction location.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain

Instruction contents travel via MDR to CIR, while sequential PC advances to 41.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

Two independent jobs take 5 and 3 minutes. Calculate ideal two-core completion time.

Sequential: 5+3 = 8
Concurrent: max(5,3) = 5
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

Each available core receives a separate independent job.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain

Ideal duration is the longer job, five minutes; serial execution would take eight.

Foundation · 5 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

State what PC, MAR, MDR, CIR and ACC contain or do.

WORKED ANSWER

[5] PC next instruction address [1], MAR memory address [1], MDR transferred data/instruction [1], CIR current instruction [1], ACC interim arithmetic result [1].

Core · 6 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

Explain how cache and extra cores could help video rendering, and why speedup may be less than expected.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Cache can reduce some slow main-memory accesses [2]; cores can process independent frames or tiles [2]; dependencies, bandwidth and overhead reduce ideal gains [2].

Challenge · 6 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

Compare a multicore CPU and GPU for an interactive logic controller and a million independent pixels.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] General control with branching suits CPU [2]; similar pixel calculations suit many GPU lanes [2]; data transfer and parallelisation overhead determine actual suitability [2].
