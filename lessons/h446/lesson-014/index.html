WORKED EXAMPLE

Turn move 8 into a board position

Convert the player's 1–9 choice into zero-based indexes.

move = 8
Worked example
STEP 1 · ZERO-BASE

Subtract 1

move - 1 = 7

Board indexes begin at 0.

STEP 2 · ROW

Integer divide by 3

7 / 3 = 2
row = 2

Integer division discards the remainder.

STEP 3 · COLUMN

Use MOD 3

7 % 3 = 1
col = 1

The remainder identifies the column.

STEP 4 · USE

Place the player

board[2,1] = player;

Move 8 maps to row 2, column 1.
