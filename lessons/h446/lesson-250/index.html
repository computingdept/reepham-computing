WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

Unmarked example: identify the appropriate search for sorted data when many lookups are required.

Sorted array → binary search
Worst-case O(log n)
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Sorted input satisfies binary search's precondition.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the answer

Each unsuccessful comparison approximately halves the search interval, producing O(log n) worst-case time.

Q1 · 10 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

(a) Trace stack Push(4), Push(7), Pop(), Push(2); give final stack. [4] (b) Trace first Lomuto partition on [7,2,6,1,5] using last pivot, giving swaps and pivot index. [6]

WORKED ANSWER

MARK SCHEME /10: (a) [4,7] [1]; pop removes 7 [1]; push 2 [1]; final [4,2] bottom-to-top [1]. (b) Pivot 5 [1]; move 2 to front [1] giving [2,7,6,1,5] [1]; move 1 into left partition giving [2,1,6,7,5] [1]; final pivot swap gives [2,1,5,7,6] [1]; pivot index2 [1].

Q2 · 10 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

(a) Independent jobs take 5 and 3 minutes; reporting takes 2 after both. Calculate ideal sequential and parallel times. [4] (b) Explain why the phrase 'make the perfect timetable' is not yet a computable requirement and give three precise details needed. [6]

WORKED ANSWER

MARK SCHEME /10: (a) sequential 5+3+2=10 [2], parallel max(5,3)+2=7 [2]. (b) 'perfect' undefined [1]; identify measurable criterion [1]; required classes/teachers [1]; available rooms/times [1]; clash-free constraints [1]; testable output/success condition [1].

Q3 · 10 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

(a) Explain why an Agile or RAD approach may suit a website needing frequent user feedback, including a drawback. [4] (b) An age check must accept 12–18 inclusive but uses `age>12 || age<18`. Explain the error, supply corrected C# condition and give four boundary-related test outcomes. [6]

WORKED ANSWER

MARK SCHEME /10: (a) Agile/RAD allows frequent revisions and feedback [2]; changing requirements accommodated [1]; scope/speed/testing risk [1]. (b) The expression age>12 || age<18 is always true for a numeric age, so it incorrectly accepts invalid ages; OR must be AND and the endpoints inclusive [1]; corrected `age>=12 && age<=18` [1]; 11 false [1],12 true [1],18 true [1],19 false [1].
