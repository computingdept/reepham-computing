Boolean identities and De Morgan's laws

By the end, you can…
  • Apply De Morgan's laws correctly
  • Simplify expressions using Boolean identities
  • Verify that two expressions agree for every input

Today's vocabulary

Boolean identityDe Morgan's lawcomplementdouble negationequivalence
EXAMPLE FIRST

Read, predict and explain

NOT(A AND B) = (NOT A) OR (NOT B)
NOT(A OR B)  = (NOT A) AND (NOT B)
STEP 1 OF 3

Negate the inputs

Distribute NOT across the brackets by negating A and B separately.

STEP 2 OF 3

Swap the operator

When applying De Morgan's law, AND changes to OR; OR changes to AND.

STEP 3 OF 3

Check equivalence

Both expressions produce the same output for all four combinations of A and B.

I DO · MODEL

Simplify ¬(A ∨ B) and verify its truth table.

¬(A ∨ B) = ¬A ∧ ¬B
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict and explain

Negate A and B separately and replace OR with AND.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and extend

Both expressions are true only when A=0 and B=0, proving equivalence.

WE DO · DIFFERENT GUIDED PROBLEM

Simplify ¬(A ∧ ¬B) without drawing a gate diagram.

¬(A ∧ ¬B) = ¬A ∨ ¬¬B = ?
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict and explain

De Morgan gives ¬A ∨ ¬(¬B).

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and extend

Double negation gives ¬A ∨ B. The two expressions have matching outputs.

Foundation · QUESTION 1 OF 3

Simplify A ∨ 0.

MODEL ANSWER

A. OR with false does not change the result.

Core · QUESTION 2 OF 3

Use De Morgan to simplify ¬(P ∧ Q).

MODEL ANSWER

¬P ∨ ¬Q. Negate each input and change AND to OR.

Stretch · QUESTION 3 OF 3

Simplify ¬(A ∨ ¬B) and justify the final result.

MODEL ANSWER

¬A ∧ B. De Morgan gives ¬A ∧ ¬¬B; double negation yields B.

TERM 1 OF 5

Boolean identity

Rule for simplifying logical expressions.

TERM 2 OF 5

De Morgan's law

Negate each operand and swap AND and OR.

TERM 3 OF 5

complement

Logical NOT of a value or expression.

TERM 4 OF 5

double negation

¬¬A simplifies to A.

TERM 5 OF 5

equivalence

Two expressions give the same output for every combination of inputs.

True or false?

De Morgan's law allows us to remove a NOT from brackets without changing AND or OR.

Answer

False. AND and OR must swap when the NOT is distributed.
