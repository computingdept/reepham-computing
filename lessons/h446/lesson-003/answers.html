Working and marking guidance
  1. 00001101 (1), 13 (1).
  2. 01000010 (1). The leftmost 1 is shifted out and discarded (1); 161 × 2 = 322 exceeds 255, while the stored value is 66 (1).
  3. 00000111 (1). The stored value is 7, not 7.5.
