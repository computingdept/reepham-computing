I DO · WORKED MODEL

A sequential fetch starts PC=120. Explain what MAR, MDR and CIR do.

PC120→MAR120; instruction arrives via MDR→CIR; sequential PC121.
WORKED EXPLANATION

Explain the method

MAR holds address, MDR transfers contents, and CIR holds instruction for decoding.

WE DO · DIFFERENT EXAMPLE

Show all steps for 8-bit two's complement −6.

00000110 → invert11111001 → add1 11111010.
WORKED EXPLANATION

Explain the method

Use exactly eight bits; inversion alone represents the wrong number.

EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

State the distinct purpose of the data bus, address bus and control bus. [3]

GIVEN INFORMATION

A small animation studio is upgrading its computers. Its processor communicates through three kinds of bus.

OCR H446/01 · June 2025 · Q1(a) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Data bus: transfers data or instructions between computer components. Address bus: carries the location of a memory address being accessed. Control bus: carries control or coordination signals.

Marking: Award one mark per correctly described bus, up to three. Do not credit a function assigned to the wrong bus.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

Compare two different technical characteristics of these approaches. [4]

GIVEN INFORMATION

A robotics company is evaluating a RISC processor against a CISC processor.

OCR H446/01 · June 2025 · Q2(a)(iii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Examples of paired contrasts: RISC has a smaller/simple instruction set versus the larger/complex CISC set; RISC instructions commonly need fewer cycles versus complex CISC instructions that may use more; RISC may be easier to pipeline whereas CISC may be harder; RISC hardware tends to be less complex.

Marking: Award two marks per full, same-feature RISC-versus-CISC comparison; maximum four. Listing unrelated statements without a matched contrast does not earn full comparison credit.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 4 · 1 MARK · 1 MINUTE

A meteorological sensor represents a signed reading using eight-bit two's complement. Convert the denary value −124 into an eight-bit two's-complement binary word. [1]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q4(a)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 1 MARKS

1000 0100.

Marking: One mark for the exact 8-bit pattern 10000100.

EXAM QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

An environmental data logger stores a positive quantity in an unnormalised signed floating-point form: eight-bit two's-complement mantissa 00001011 and four-bit two's-complement exponent 0111. Re-express this value as a normalised floating-point number in the SAME eight-plus-four-bit format. Show the mantissa shifts and adjusted exponent. [4]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q4(d) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Normalise positive mantissa until it starts with 01: shift 00001011 left by three positions to 01011000. Decrease the exponent 7 by 3 to 4, written 0100. Final: 01011000 0100.

Marking: One mark for reducing exponent by three to 4; one for the mantissa starting 01011...; one for the completed eight-bit mantissa 01011000; one for four-bit exponent 0100. Correct two fields with valid binary working may earn all four.
