The practical goal is not memorising method names. It is understanding what each operation means for each structure.
STEP 1 OF 4

Lists and linear structures

List: Add / Remove / foreach
Stack: Push / Pop
Queue: Enqueue / Dequeue

Operations reflect each structure's ordering rule.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Linked structures

Linked list: change Next
BST: change Left / Right

References must be updated without breaking access to the remaining nodes.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Network structure

Graph: add/remove edge
Traverse: inspect connected vertices

Directed and undirected graphs differ in whether one or two matrix entries change.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Hashed structure

index = Hash(key)
add/search/remove inside bucket

Hashing chooses where to work before the bucket operation happens.
