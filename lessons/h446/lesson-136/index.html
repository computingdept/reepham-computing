MODEL ANSWER

QUESTION 2 OF 4

What is a composite key?

MODEL ANSWER

MODEL ANSWER

On a match or after examining all items.

MODEL ANSWER

TERM 1/4

1NF

Attributes hold atomic values and rows can be uniquely identified.

TERM 2/4

2NF

In 1NF, no non-key attribute depends on only part of a composite candidate key.

TERM 3/4

partial dependency

Non-key field depends on a subset of a composite key.

TERM 4/4

anomaly

Insertion, update or deletion causes undesirable effects.

True or false?

A relation with a single-attribute candidate key can have a partial dependency on that key.

Model answer

False. Partial-key dependency requires a composite candidate key.
