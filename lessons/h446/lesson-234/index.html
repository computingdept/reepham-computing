NEW LEARNING · EXAMPLE FIRST

Study the worked scenario

// SPIRAL: each cycle evaluates risks before
// developing and validating the next version.
// RAD: develop prototypes quickly, collect
// feedback and revise the working model.
STEP 1 OF 3

Spiral focuses on risk

Each cycle plans objectives, identifies or resolves major risks, develops/tests and reviews with stakeholders.

STEP 2 OF 3

RAD is prototype-led

Users evaluate quickly produced models. Frequent prototyping accelerates understanding and can reveal unclear requirements.

STEP 3 OF 3

Compare suitability

Spiral can suit large/high-risk systems but increases management cost; RAD relies on accessible users and skilled teams and may compromise architecture if rushed.

I DO · TEACHER MODEL

A satellite control project has major safety and technology uncertainties. Recommend a method.

Identify safety risk → prototype risky control
→ assess results → refine scope → next spiral
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Spiral is appropriate because major technical and safety risks need assessment at each cycle.

STEP 2 OF 2

Model and explain

It demands experienced risk assessment and carries greater time/management overhead than simpler methods.

WE DO · DIFFERENT GUIDED PROBLEM

A café needs a quick booking interface but cannot clearly describe its desired layout. Recommend a method.

Sketch UI → build prototype → café reviews
→ revise → pilot working version
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

RAD suits evolving interface requirements because the café can see and comment on working prototypes.

STEP 2 OF 2

Model and explain

A risk is that repeated quick changes may weaken maintainability without time for robust design.

Foundation · 4 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

State one key characteristic and one disadvantage of both Spiral and RAD.

WORKED ANSWER

[4] Spiral is risk-driven [1], but adds assessment/management cost [1]; RAD creates prototypes with user feedback [1], but rapid changes can compromise design quality [1].

Core · 6 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

A transport company is building an experimental driverless control system. Compare Spiral with RAD and justify a choice.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Spiral explicitly assesses critical risks [1] and validates feasibility each cycle [1]; RAD rapidly gathers interface feedback [1], which is less central than safety risks [1]; choose Spiral [1] because controlling safety/technical uncertainty matters more than fast UI prototyping [1].

Stretch · 6 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

A booking website must launch rapidly and users are available daily. Explain three benefits and two risks of RAD.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Early working prototypes [1]; frequent direct feedback [1]; requirements clarified quickly [1]; helps prioritise useful features [1]; users must be available [1] and rushed design/testing can undermine quality [1].

True or false?

Spiral development and RAD are identical because both can repeat stages.

Model answer

False. Spiral is explicitly risk-driven; RAD centres on rapid prototyping and frequent user feedback.
