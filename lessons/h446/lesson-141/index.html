I DO · WORKED MODEL

A transfer subtracts £20 from Account A but crashes before adding £20 to Account B. Which ACID property matters most?

STEP 1 OF 3

Subtract £20 from A

Identify the relevant information and make a prediction.

STEP 2 OF 3

Crash before adding to B

Explain the change using correct technical terms.

STEP 3 OF 3

Rollback both changes

Atomicity means the transfer is all-or-nothing. A DBMS transaction can roll back partial changes. Consistency checks valid constraints; isolation keeps concurrent transfers safe; durability preserves committed transfers.

True or false?

RAID or replication guarantees recovery after an accidental DELETE.

Model answer

False. Replicated deletions can propagate; an independent backup is still needed.
