Which algorithm fits?

STEP 1 OF 4

Match the algorithm to the task

Quicksort partitions an array. Dijkstra finds shortest routes on non-negative weighted graphs. A* adds a remaining-cost estimate.

STEP 2 OF 4

Show all required intermediate states

For quicksort write arrays after partition. For shortest paths maintain distances, predecessors and a clear visited or frontier order.

STEP 3 OF 4

State when each algorithm is appropriate and how its method changes work.

STEP 4 OF 4

Exam command words matter

Trace = show states. Explain = give a cause or reason. Compare = connect similarities and differences with evidence.

TERM 1 OF 5

partition

Rearrange around a pivot and place that pivot.

TERM 2 OF 5

tentative distance

Current best-known cost from the start.

TERM 3 OF 5

predecessor

Vertex immediately before another on a path.

TERM 4 OF 5

heuristic

Estimate of remaining distance to the goal.

TERM 5 OF 5

admissibility

A heuristic never exceeds the true cheapest remaining cost.

True or false?

Quicksort, Dijkstra and A* are interchangeable because they all have a loop.

Model answer

False. They address different problems: ordering values, shortest paths and heuristic-guided search respectively.
