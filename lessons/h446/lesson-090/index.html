Lesson 090 · Inside City Hall: ALU, Control Unit and Registers

By the end you can…
  • Control Unit generates control signals and interprets the fetched instruction. It is not the component that performs arithmetic itself.
  • ALU completes arithmetic and logic such as addition and comparisons; the ACC may hold a result in an accumulator-style model.
  • Registers serve distinct roles: PC next instruction, MAR memory address, MDR transferred contents, CIR current instruction, ACC intermediate calculation.
