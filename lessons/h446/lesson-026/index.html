OR gate. Q is 1 if A or B (or both) is 1.

An OR gate also outputs 1 when both inputs are 1.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

Retrieval

Complete the missing output Q.

ANSWER

Q = 0.

XOR is 1 only when exactly one input is 1.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Retrieval

Write the Boolean expression for Q.

ANSWER

Q = ¬(A ∧ B).

Trace left to right: AND first, then NOT the result (NAND).

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Challenge

An alarm sounds if the door is open AND the system is armed, OR if panic is pressed. Write Q using A, B and C.

ANSWER

Q = (A ∧ B) ∨ C.

Group A AND B first, then OR with C. Sketch an AND gate feeding an OR gate.

1.4.3(c) · BOOLEAN ALGEBRA

Change form without changing truth

De Morgan:
¬(A ∧ B) = ¬A ∨ ¬B
¬(A ∨ B) = ¬A ∧ ¬B

Double negation:
¬¬A = A
Name the rule, apply one change at a time, and keep the expression logically equivalent.
STEP 1 OF 4

De Morgan

¬(A ∧ B) → ¬A ∨ ¬B
¬(A ∨ B) → ¬A ∧ ¬B

The operator inside the brackets swaps as each term is negated.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Double negation

¬(¬B) → B

Two consecutive NOT operations cancel.

STEP 3 OF 4

Distribution

(A ∨ B) ∧ (A ∨ C)
→ A ∨ (B ∧ C)

OCR explicitly tests this form.

STEP 4 OF 4

Association + commutation

A ∨ (B ∨ C)
= (A ∨ B) ∨ C

A ∧ B
= B ∧ A

These allow regrouping and reordering before other simplification.
