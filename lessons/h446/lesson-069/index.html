Lesson 069 · Data Mining and Visualisation of Patterns

By the end you can…
  • Data mining seeks patterns or associations across sets of data; define the question, prepare records and examine the results.
  • Visualisation makes patterns and anomalies visible. Scatter charts suit pairs of numeric data; bar charts compare categories.
  • A correlation does not establish cause. Sampling, confounders, missing values and measurement choices may mislead interpretation.
