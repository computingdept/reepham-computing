STEP 1 OF 3

Define the heuristic

A heuristic quickly estimates how promising a choice is; it is not automatically an exact solution.

STEP 2 OF 3

Model the trade-off

Separate local edge cost, accumulated cost and estimated remaining cost before choosing a path.

STEP 3 OF 3

Pipeline and performance models

A pipeline can overlap independent stages, increasing throughput, but dependencies and resource constraints may cause stalls.

I DO · WORKED MODEL

Evaluate two routes A–B–G (3+7) and A–C–G (5+1).

Via B: 3+7=10
Via C: 5+1=6
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Calculate complete route costs, rather than deciding only from the first edge.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the method

The via-C path is cheaper at six total units despite a larger first edge.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

A three-stage pipeline receives four independent tasks, one task per stage per cycle. When does the fourth finish?

Stages S1,S2,S3
Task4 starts S1 in cycle4
Finishes S3 in cycle6
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Task one occupies S1 in cycle 1, with one new task entering each later cycle.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the method

Task four completes stage S3 in cycle 6, assuming no stalls; it is not completed in cycle 4.
