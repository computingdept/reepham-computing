Original question · 1 of 4CORE · 2 VARIABLES

1. Two groups

Circle the two largest pairs. One square can be in both pairs. Which input remains 1 in each group? Write Q and name its gate.

Worked solution · 1 of 4
FINAL ANSWER
Q = A ∨ B

Bottom row: A stays 1; B changes. Keep A.

Right column: B stays 1; A changes. Keep B.

Join with OR: Either pair can make Q=1.

Gate: A and B feed one OR gate.

Original question · 2 of 4CORE · 3 VARIABLES

2. One pair with NOT

Circle the two 1s. Rows are A; columns are BC. Identify the two inputs that stay fixed. Write Q as one AND term.

Worked solution · 2 of 4
FINAL ANSWER
Q = ¬A ∧ B

Circle: Two 1s in the top row under BC=11 and BC=10.

Compare: A stays 0, B stays 1, and C changes.

Write: Keep NOT A (¬A) and B, joined by AND. Leave C out.

Gates: NOT A, then AND with B.

Original question · 3 of 4APPLY · GRAY-CODE EDGES

3. One group across both edges

The first and last columns are neighbours. Circle the four 1s as one group. Which input stays 0? What does Q simplify to?

Worked solution · 3 of 4
FINAL ANSWER
Q = ¬C

One wraparound group: Both A rows at BC=00 and BC=10. The left and right edges touch.

Compare: A and B change, while C always stays 0.

Write: Remove A and B; retain ¬C.

Gate: One NOT gate with input C.

4. Simplify, then plan the gates

This practice map is reconstructed from the four-variable expression used earlier in this lesson. Circle two groups of four, including the wraparound group. For each, compare the inputs, write a term and join the terms with OR. Finally plan the circuit for P.

Worked solution · 4 of 4
FINAL ANSWER
P = (¬A ∧ ¬C) ∨ (A ∧ ¬D)

Orange, top-left group of four: AB=00/01; CD=00/01. A stays 0 and C stays 0. B and D change. First term = ¬A ∧ ¬C.

Blue, bottom wraparound group of four: AB=11/10; CD=00/10. A stays 1 and D stays 0. B and C change. Second term = A ∧ ¬D.

Join: P is 1 when either term is true, so use OR.

Gate plan: NOT A, NOT C → AND; A, NOT D → AND; then OR the two outputs. B is not needed.
