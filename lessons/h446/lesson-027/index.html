1.4.3(b) · KARNAUGH MAPS

Keep only variables that stay constant

Columns AB: 00 01 11 10
Rows    CD: 00 01 11 10

Group 4 or 8 where possible.
Changing variable → disappears.
Constant variable → remains.
The map is Gray-code ordered. Opposite edges touch, so do not treat it like an ordinary table.
STEP 1 OF 4

Valid groups

1, 2, 4, 8, 16 cells
rectangles only
all cells = 1
largest useful groups first

Diagonal cells are not adjacent.

Derive a term

If A=0 and C=0 throughout group:
term = ¬A ∧ ¬C

B and D disappear because they vary.

term1 ∨ term2

STEP 1 · A

STEP 2 · B

Remove B

B changes → omit B
STEP 3 · C

Keep C negated

C = 0 throughout → ¬C
STEP 4 · D

Remove D

D changes → omit D

term = ¬A ∧ ¬C
