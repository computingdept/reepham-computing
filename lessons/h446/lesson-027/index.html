QUESTION 1 OF 4

TWO’S COMPLEMENT

Write −13 as an 8-bit two’s-complement integer.

ANSWER

11110011.

Start with +13 = 00001101. Invert the bits → 11110010, then add 1 → 11110011.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

DE MORGAN

For the circuit shown, simplify Q = ¬(A ∨ B) using De Morgan’s Law.

ANSWER

Q = ¬A ∧ ¬B.

NOT of an OR becomes AND of the individually negated inputs. Both expressions are equivalent.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

BUBBLE SORT

Complete one full left-to-right pass of ascending bubble sort. What is the resulting array?

ANSWER

[4, 6, 2, 8, 9].

Compare neighbouring items and swap where needed: [4,9,6,2,8] → [4,6,9,2,8] → [4,6,2,9,8] → [4,6,2,8,9].

QUESTION 4 OF 4

CHALLENGE · NORMALISATION

Normalise this positive binary mantissa without changing its value. Give the new exponent.

ANSWER

0.110100 × 2².

Shift the mantissa point two places right (0.001101 → 0.110100), so decrease the exponent from 4 to 2. The numerical value stays the same.

1.4.3(b) · KARNAUGH MAPS

Read the map first

Rows and columns use Gray code. Adjacent cells differ in one bit; the opposite edges also touch.
STEP 1 OF 4

Fill the grid

For each row and column, evaluate the Boolean expression. Put 1 where the output is true and 0 otherwise.

Columns AB: 00 01 11 10
Rows CD:       00 01 11 10
1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Group adjacent 1s

Use rectangles containing 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 cells. Make the largest useful groups. Overlap is allowed.

Diagonals are not adjacent; edges wrap around.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Keep the constants

Write the term for each group. A constant 0 becomes NOT; a constant 1 stays positive. Variables that change disappear.

A stays 0 → ¬A
C stays 0 → ¬C
term = ¬A ∧ ¬C
3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

OR the group terms

Each group gives an AND term. Use OR (∨) to combine them.

group 1 ∨ group 2

Check that every 1 is covered and that the expression agrees with the map.

ADAPTED FROM OCR Q2(b) · FREE POPCORN

From an expression to two groups

Voucher E plus a soft drink F or water G: R = (E ∧ F) ∨ (E ∧ G). Represent this on a K-map.

Worked example · E is the row; FG are the columns.
STEP 1 · FILL

Complete the map

Use FG in Gray-code order: 00, 01, 11, 10. For E=0 the output is always 0. For E=1 the values are 0, 1, 1, 1.

STEP 2 · GROUP ONE

Group E ∧ F

Pair cells FG=11 and FG=10 in row E=1. E and F stay 1; G changes.

Group 1 = E ∧ F
STEP 3 · GROUP TWO

Group E ∧ G

Pair FG=01 and FG=11 in row E=1. E and G stay 1; F changes. The shared cell is allowed in both groups.

Group 2 = E ∧ G
STEP 4 · CHECK

Combine and factor

R = (E ∧ F) ∨ (E ∧ G)
  = E ∧ (F ∨ G)

The K-map shows the two terms. Factoring gives the form in OCR's Q2(b) mark scheme.
