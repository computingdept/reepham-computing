QUESTION 1 OF 4

NORMALISED NUMBERS

Normalise 0.0110 × 2⁴ using a two’s-complement mantissa. Give the new mantissa and exponent.

ANSWER

0.1100 × 2³.

Shift the mantissa one place left and decrease the exponent by one to preserve the value. A normalised positive two’s-complement mantissa begins 01; a negative one begins 10.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

BINARY SEARCH

A sorted array is [3, 8, 12, 19, 27, 35, 42]. When searching for 27, which indexes are checked, in order?

ANSWER

Indexes 3 → 5 → 4 (values 19 → 35 → 27).

Binary search tests the middle index of the remaining sorted range, using zero-based indexing.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

BUBBLE SORT

Show the array after one full left-to-right pass of ascending bubble sort on [7, 3, 5, 2].

ANSWER

[3, 5, 2, 7].

Compare adjacent values and swap when the left value is larger. The largest value, 7, moves to the end.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

CHALLENGE · APPLY

An alarm sounds if the door is open AND the system is armed, OR if panic is pressed. Write Q using A, B and C.

ANSWER

Q = (A ∧ B) ∨ C.

A = door open, B = armed, C = panic pressed. Group door AND armed first; panic is an alternative trigger.

1.4.3(c) · BOOLEAN ALGEBRA

Change form without changing truth

De Morgan:
¬(A ∧ B) = ¬A ∨ ¬B
¬(A ∨ B) = ¬A ∧ ¬B

Double negation:
¬¬A = A
Name the rule, apply one change at a time, and keep the expression logically equivalent.
STEP 1 OF 4

De Morgan

¬(A ∧ B) → ¬A ∨ ¬B
¬(A ∨ B) → ¬A ∧ ¬B

The operator inside the brackets swaps as each term is negated.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Double negation

¬(¬B) → B

Two consecutive NOT operations cancel.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Distributive law

(A ∧ B) ∨ (A ∧ C)
= A ∧ (B ∨ C)

A appears in both groups. Write it once outside the brackets, then join B and C with OR.

The matching OR form is also useful: (A ∨ B) ∧ (A ∨ C) = A ∨ (B ∧ C).

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Association + commutation

A ∨ (B ∨ C)
= (A ∨ B) ∨ C

A ∧ B
= B ∧ A

These allow regrouping and reordering before other simplification.

OCR 2019 · Q5(i–iii) · 3 MARKS

Simplify Boolean expressions

State the simplified form of each expression:

(i) ¬¬A   (ii) ¬A ∧ ¬B   (iii) ¬(¬A ∧ ¬B)

Apply double negation and De Morgan’s laws. Show each step.
WORKED ANSWER 1 OF 4

(i) Double negation

¬¬A = A

Two NOT operations cancel.

(ii) De Morgan’s law

¬A ∧ ¬B = ¬(A ∨ B)

NOT A AND NOT B means neither A nor B is true. This is an equivalent expression, not necessarily a shorter one.

(iii) De Morgan’s law, then double negation

¬(¬A ∧ ¬B)
= ¬¬A ∨ ¬¬B
= A ∨ B
OCR 2019 · Q2(b)(ii) · 2 MARKS

Simplify the popcorn expression

A cinema gives free popcorn when a voucher is shown and either a soft drink or a bottle of water is purchased.

E = voucher shown; F = soft drink bought; G = water bought.

Simplify (E ∧ F) ∨ (E ∧ G).

Look for a common factor. Keep the meaning unchanged.
WORKED ANSWER 2 OF 4

Step 1 — identify the repeated factor E.

(E ∧ F) ∨ (E ∧ G)

Step 2 — use the distributive law.

= E ∧ (F ∨ G)

Read it back: the voucher must be shown AND either a soft drink OR water must be purchased.

OCR 2019 · Q2(a) · 3 MARKS

Cinema discount: Boolean expression

A discounted cinema ticket is issued if the customer is under 18 AND has a student card, OR is over 60 AND has proof of age.

A = under 18; B = student card; C = over 60; D = ID. Write the Boolean expression for the output Q (discount ticket).

Group each pair of conditions before joining the alternatives.
WORKED ANSWER 3 OF 4

Step 1 — under-18 route: under 18 AND student card.

A ∧ B

Step 2 — over-60 route: over 60 AND ID.

C ∧ D

Step 3 — either route qualifies, so combine with OR.

Q = (A ∧ B) ∨ (C ∧ D)
OCR 2019 · Q3(ii) · 2 MARKS

From a gate circuit to an expression

Two OR gates take inputs A, B and C, D respectively. Their outputs feed a third OR gate. Write the complete Boolean expression for the circuit output.

Represent each first-stage gate in brackets, then combine the results.
WORKED ANSWER 4 OF 4

Step 1 — first OR gate:

X = A ∨ B

Step 2 — second OR gate:

Y = C ∨ D

Step 3 — final OR gate joins X and Y:

Q = X ∨ Y
  = (A ∨ B) ∨ (C ∨ D)

Because OR is associative, this also equals A ∨ B ∨ C ∨ D.
