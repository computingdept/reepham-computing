Original question · 1 of 6ADAPTED FROM OCR Q6 · FULL ADDER

1. Find the carry expression

Circle the largest groups of 1s for Cout, then give the simplified sum of products. [4]

Three pairs; every output 1 is covered.
Completed answer
FINAL ANSWER
Cout = (A ∧ B) ∨ (A ∧ Cin) ∨ (B ∧ Cin)
Working and marking guidance

Cout = (A ∧ B) ∨ (A ∧ Cin) ∨ (B ∧ Cin)

Pairs are: row A=1, columns 11/10; row A=1, columns 01/11; and column 11 in both rows. This is equivalent to OCR's circuit expression ((A XOR B) ∧ Cin) ∨ (A ∧ B).

Original question · 2 of 6ADAPTED FROM OCR Q5(iii) · DE MORGAN

2. Verify with a 2-variable map

The original expression is ¬(¬A ∧ ¬B). Use the map to simplify it. [3]

Show two groups and give the final expression.
Completed answer
FINAL ANSWER
A ∨ B
Working and marking guidance

Groups along A=1 and B=1 give A ∨ B.

So ¬(¬A ∧ ¬B) = A ∨ B. Both groups overlap at A=1, B=1.

3. The four corners are adjacent

Draw a single 4-cell group using the opposite edges. Write the simplified term. [3]

Check which variables stay constant across all four corners.
Completed answer
FINAL ANSWER
¬B ∧ ¬D
Working and marking guidance

Columns AB=00 and 10 have B=0; rows CD=00 and 10 have D=0. A and C vary.

Q = ¬B ∧ ¬D.

Original question · 5 of 6ADAPTED FROM OCR Q1 · HALF ADDER

5. Represent SUM and CARRY

Draw the two K-maps for the half adder. Simplify SUM (use OCR's ∨ for XOR) and CARRY. [4]

Diagonal SUM cells cannot be grouped as neighbours.
Completed answer
FINAL ANSWER
SUM = A ∨ B · CARRY = A ∧ B
Working and marking guidance

SUM = A ∨ B (XOR in OCR's underlined-∨ notation); CARRY = A ∧ B.

The diagonal SUM 1s are not adjacent on the map: they remain as separate terms (¬A ∧ B) ∨ (A ∧ ¬B), or XOR.

Original question · 6 of 6ADAPTED FROM OCR Q3 · LOGIC CIRCUIT

6. Challenge: four-input OR

Every combination except 0000 produces 1. Find groups and simplify the output. [4]

Try four groups of eight; a variable changing within a group is removed.
Completed answer
FINAL ANSWER
A ∨ B ∨ C ∨ D
Working and marking guidance

Four valid groups of eight cover all 1s: A=1, B=1, C=1 and D=1.

Output = A ∨ B ∨ C ∨ D.
