I DO · MODEL

A pupil claims bubble-sort pass 1 on [6,2,5,1] gives [2,5,6,1]. Diagnose the missed step.

Compare 6 with 2, then 5, then 1.
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

[2,5,6,1] is an intermediate state; the last comparison 6 versus 1 is still outstanding.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check the method

Swap 6 and 1. Correct pass-1 end is [2,5,1,6].

WE DO · DIFFERENT EXAMPLE

A pupil normalises 0.0010₂ × 2⁵ to 0.1000₂ × 2⁵. Identify and correct the error.

Two mantissa left shifts require two exponent decreases.
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Shifting the mantissa left increases its value. The exponent must decrease by the same number of positions.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check the method

Two shifts give 0.1000₂ × 2³, not ×2⁵, and preserve the decimal value.

GIVEN INFORMATION

A forest study centre inserts the following integer tags into an initially empty binary search tree in this order: 42, 24, 12, 68, 75, 31, 17. Smaller values go left; larger values go right.

OCR H446/02 · June 2023 · Q1(a)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Root 42; left child 24, right child 68. Children of 24: 12 left, 31 right. 12 has right child 17. 68 has right child 75.

Marking: Four grouped marks adapted to this RHS tree: correct root (1), both immediate children (1), the two children of 24 (1), both lower placements 17 and 75 (1).

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 3 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

The same rescue centre improves its bubble sort by recording whether a swap happened in each pass. Explain how this can allow the algorithm to stop early, rather than always performing its maximum number of passes. [2]

OCR H446/02 · June 2025 · Q1(a)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

Reset the Boolean swap flag at the start of each pass. Set it when any two items are exchanged; if the pass finishes with the flag still false, there were no swaps so all items are in order and further passes stop.

Marking: One mark for tracking whether swaps occurred, one for stopping when the most recent complete pass had no swaps. A reference to the flag or equivalent condition is required.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 3 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

An environmental data logger stores a positive quantity in an unnormalised signed floating-point form: eight-bit two's-complement mantissa 00001011 and four-bit two's-complement exponent 0111. Re-express this value as a normalised floating-point number in the SAME eight-plus-four-bit format. Show the mantissa shifts and adjusted exponent. [4]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q4(d) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Normalise positive mantissa until it starts with 01: shift 00001011 left by three positions to 01011000. Decrease the exponent 7 by 3 to 4, written 0100. Final: 01011000 0100.

Marking: One mark for reducing exponent by three to 4; one for the mantissa starting 01011...; one for the completed eight-bit mantissa 01011000; one for four-bit exponent 0100. Correct two fields with valid binary working may earn all four.
