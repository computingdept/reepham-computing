Applications, utilities and source licensing

By the end you can…
  • Application software provides end-user capabilities; utilities perform system maintenance, protection or management.
  • Open-source means qualifying licences grant rights to inspect, change and redistribute source code under terms.
  • Proprietary licensing can restrict modification. Compare cost, support, compatibility, accessibility and staff skills.
