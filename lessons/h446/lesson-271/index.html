Applications, utilities and source licensing

By the end you can…
  • Application software provides end-user capabilities; utilities perform system maintenance, protection or management.
  • Open-source means qualifying licences grant rights to inspect, change and redistribute source code under terms.
  • Proprietary licensing can restrict modification. Compare cost, support, compatibility, accessibility and staff skills.

EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

Name two different utility programs that could be installed and state one purpose of each. [4]

GIVEN INFORMATION

A school technician is preparing new laptops for pupils.

OCR H446/01 · June 2025 · Q1(e) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Examples: backup copies files for recovery; antivirus detects/protects against malware; compression reduces storage required; encryption makes stored information unreadable without the right key; disk cleanup removes unnecessary files; file repair restores damaged files.

Marking: For each of two different utilities: one mark for the named utility and one for its valid purpose. Four marks maximum; avoid counting the same utility twice.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

The bakery already uses a spreadsheet. Name TWO other TYPES of application software suitable for this business. For each type, give one distinct task the bakery could complete with it. [4]

OCR H446/01 · June 2023 · Q1(a)(ii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Examples: DBMS to maintain customer/supplier records; word processor for employment letters; DTP for menus; graphics editor for adverts; presentation software for staff training. No brand required.

Marking: Two marks per suitable software type + business-specific task; no spreadsheet (given); don't award a standalone brand name as software type.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

The bakery uses paid closed-source software to manage ingredients. Describe three possible drawbacks to the business of choosing closed-source software rather than open-source software. [3]

OCR H446/01 · June 2023 · Q1(a)(iii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

The business may need licences or subscription fees; cannot inspect/change the proprietary source itself; cannot fix a bug or add specialised features without the vendor; may become dependent on vendor updates/support.

Marking: One mark per different drawback, max three. Do not merely list the advantages of open source in reverse without a closed-source consequence.

EXAM QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 1 MARK · 1 MINUTE

A small bakery uses a spreadsheet to calculate its weekly staff wages. Give one benefit of using formulas rather than completing each wage calculation by hand. [1]

OCR H446/01 · June 2023 · Q1(a)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 1 MARKS

Copying a tested formula recalculates many wages automatically when hours or rates change; this reduces repeated manual calculation and errors.

Marking: One mark for a concrete, developed spreadsheet advantage such as automatic recalculation, applying a copied formula, improved accuracy or what-if analysis. Bare 'faster' needs explanation.
