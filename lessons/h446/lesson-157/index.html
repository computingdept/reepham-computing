QUESTION 1 OF 4

Why must a server validate data sent by a browser?

MODEL ANSWER

Client-side checks can be bypassed, so authoritative checks belong on the server.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

What is the purpose of an operating system's memory management?

MODEL ANSWER

Allocating and protecting memory for running programs.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

What is the worst-case order of binary search?

MODEL ANSWER

O(log n) on sorted random-access data.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Why does the TLS padlock not guarantee a site is honest?

MODEL ANSWER

Encryption in transit does not establish trustworthiness of operator.

I DO · MODELLED APPLICATION

A school stores student medical notes alongside general attendance records and permits every staff member to open them. Identify data-protection issues.

STEP 1 OF 3

Identify sensitive data

Identify the relevant facts, purpose and authority.

STEP 2 OF 3

Restrict justified access

Discuss which law or safeguard fits the situation.

STEP 3 OF 3

Secure, minimise and review

Medical information is sensitive personal data with stronger protections. Restrict access by legitimate role, use a proper lawful basis and relevant special-category condition, minimise collected fields, keep information accurate and protect access. DPA 1998 is named historically by OCR but current UK law is DPA 2018/UK GDPR.

WE DO · DIFFERENT CASE

An online revision form collects names, birthdays, home addresses and marks even though only anonymous quiz feedback is needed. What should change?

STEP 1 OF 3

Quiz purpose identified

STEP 2 OF 3

Discuss which law or safeguard fits the situation.

STEP 3 OF 3

Give clear privacy notice

Under data minimisation, only collect what is needed for the stated function; avoid unnecessary personal identifiers, explain the purpose, set retention and secure any necessary records. A school also considers transparency and the rights of data subjects.
