QUESTION 1 OF 4

Index 0

In an array A, what does A[0] refer to?

ANSWER

The first element in the array.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

Iteration

Which construct is used to repeat instructions?

ANSWER

A loop, such as for, while or do...until.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Temporary value

Why might a temporary variable be needed when swapping two values?

ANSWER

It stores one value so it is not overwritten before the swap is complete.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Stopping early

What would make a sorting algorithm stop early?

ANSWER

A complete pass in which no swaps are made shows the data is already sorted.

Key vocabulary for today
bubble sortpassadjacentswapascendingdescendingflagcomparison
TERM 1 OF 8

bubble sort

DEFINITION

A sorting algorithm that repeatedly compares adjacent items and swaps those in the wrong order.

TERM 2 OF 8

pass

DEFINITION

One complete movement through the unsorted part of the data.

TERM 3 OF 8

adjacent

DEFINITION

Next to each other in the list or array.

TERM 4 OF 8

swap

DEFINITION

Exchange the positions of two data items.

TERM 5 OF 8

ascending

DEFINITION

Ordered from smallest to largest.

TERM 6 OF 8

descending

DEFINITION

Ordered from largest to smallest.

TERM 7 OF 8

flag

DEFINITION

A Boolean variable used to record whether a condition has occurred.

TERM 8 OF 8

comparison

DEFINITION

Testing two values to decide whether their order is correct.

STANDARD ALGORITHM · SORTING

Compare → swap → complete a pass → repeat

Example list: 5  2  4  1

Compare 5 and 2 → swap
Compare 5 and 4 → swap
Compare 5 and 1 → swap

After pass 1:
2  4  1  5
Key idea: Bubble sort works on adjacent pairs. A complete pass with no swaps means the data is sorted.
