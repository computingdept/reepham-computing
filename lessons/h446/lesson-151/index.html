Type selects black

Identify the input or diagram and predict the first result.

STEP 2 OF 3

Follow the second stage and explain its importance.

STEP 3 OF 3

ID selects green

Green: matching ID selector generally outranks the class and element selectors where origin, importance and layers are equal.

WE DO · DIFFERENT CASE

A content-box element has width 180px, horizontal padding 10px each side and 2px border each side. Total outer border width?

STEP 1 OF 3

Content 180px

Identify the input or diagram and predict the first result.

STEP 2 OF 3

Padding totals 20px

Follow the second stage and explain its importance.

STEP 3 OF 3

Border totals 4px → 204

180 + 20 + 4 = 204px. Margins would add extra occupied space. With border-box sizing, declared width includes padding and border.
