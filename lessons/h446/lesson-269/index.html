QUESTION 4 OF 4

Year 12 challenge

Rising-edge flip-flop Q=0. D pulses high between edges but is 0 at next rising edge. What is new Q?

MODEL ANSWER

Q remains 0; only D at the active clock edge is sampled.

Operating-system types

By the end you can…
  • Real-time OS correctness depends on deadlines, not just average response speed.
  • Embedded and distributed refer to system architecture and purpose; each can overlap with real-time.
  • Multi-user concerns people/account isolation, whereas multitasking concerns processes.

Trace the decision

Real-time: critical deadlines.
Embedded: dedicated device.
Distributed: coordinated machines.
Multi-user: distinct users and permissions.
Multitasking: multiple processes progressing.
