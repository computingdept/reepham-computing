CODE FIRST · WORKED REPRESENTATION

Predict what changes

// Insert 25 into a BST with root 40.
// 25 < 40 → left to 20.
// 25 > 20 → right to 30.
// 25 < 30 → insert as left child of 30.
STEP 1 OF 3

Start at root

Compare the new value 25 with root 40; it belongs in the left subtree.

STEP 2 OF 3

Compare again

25 is greater than 20, so move right. The right child currently stores 30.

STEP 3 OF 3

Insert without breaking the property

25 is smaller than 30; insert it as the left child of 30.

I DO · WORKED

Insert 12 into BST with root 20, left 10 and right 30.

12 < 20 → LEFT
12 > 10 → RIGHT
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

Compare 12 with 20 → go left to 10.

STEP 2 OF 2

Confirm and explain

12 > 10, so 12 becomes the right child of 10.

WE DO · DIFFERENT TASK

Insert 35 into BST with root 25, left 15, right 40.

35 > 25 → RIGHT
35 < 40 → LEFT
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

35 is greater than 25, so go right.

STEP 2 OF 2

Confirm and explain

35 is smaller than 40, so insert it as the left child of 40.

Foundation · 1 OF 3

BST root 50, left child 25, right child 75: where does 60 go?

MODEL ANSWER

Left child of 75 because 60>50 and 60<75.

Core · 2 OF 3

Insert 15, 10, 20, 18 into an empty BST. Identify the parent of 18.

MODEL ANSWER

18 is the left child of 20, which is the right child of 15.

Stretch · 3 OF 3

Explain why a BST can have O(n) worst-case search time despite O(log n) average search on a reasonably balanced tree.

MODEL ANSWER

A sequence of sorted inserts can make it a chain of n nodes; each lookup may visit every node.

TERM 1 OF 5

root

Topmost tree node.

TERM 2 OF 5

child

Node directly below a parent.

TERM 3 OF 5

subtree

A node and all its descendants.

TERM 4 OF 5

binary search tree

A binary tree ordered by values, with smaller keys to the left and greater to the right.

TERM 5 OF 5

balanced

Height kept relatively small compared with node count.

True or false?

Every binary search tree has logarithmic worst-case lookup time.

Model answer

False. An unbalanced BST may degenerate into a chain with O(n) lookup.
