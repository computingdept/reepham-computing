STEP 3 OF 3

Standard protocols

A school LAN connects local devices. Ethernet/Wi-Fi standards support compatible links and IP-based protocols allow data exchange. A router becomes relevant for communication beyond the local IP network.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

A trust connects schools 40 miles apart using provider links. Classify the network.

STEP 1 OF 3

Separate school LANs

Identify the relevant information and make a prediction.

STEP 2 OF 3

Router/provider link

Explain the change using correct technical terms.

STEP 3 OF 3

Connected WAN

It is a WAN connecting multiple site LANs. Routers and service-provider infrastructure carry traffic between sites; secure connections and availability matter.
