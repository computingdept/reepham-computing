Lesson 073 · Trace Tables and Boundary Testing

By the end you can…
  • A trace table records variable values after each meaningful execution step: i, a[i], condition and result.
  • A boundary test probes just below, at and above a threshold. Normal, erroneous and exceptional tests reveal different faults.
  • Test early success, late success, absent target, and empty arrays. For this function, −1 distinguishes not found from index0.
