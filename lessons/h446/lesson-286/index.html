Lossy and lossless compression

By the end you can…
  • Lossless compression represents data more efficiently without discarding any information and reconstructs the original exactly.
  • Lossy methods intentionally discard information; a decoded image/audio can be perceptually similar but not bit-for-bit identical.
  • Choose lossless for text, programs, records or medical numeric originals; lossy can be appropriate for delivery copies of photos/video/audio with acceptable quality reduction.

EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 5 MARKS · 5 MINUTES

A museum streams high-definition lecture videos to visitors on different internet connections. Compare LOSSY and LOSSLESS compression, including FIVE technical and viewer-visible consequences for continuous playback. [5]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q2(f) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 5 MARKS

Lossy encoding discards information irreversibly and may cause quality loss but reduces bitrate/buffering and can adapt to weak connections. Lossless can recreate the original content exactly and preserve fidelity but commonly needs more data/bandwidth and may cause stalls.

Marking: One mark per distinct accurate comparison/streaming consequence (max five). Do not award bare 'faster downloads': the context is streaming.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

An employee types new names and dates into a thin-client application. Those text changes travel over the network to a server. Explain why lossless compression is preferable to lossy compression for sending the text. [3]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q3(c) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Lossless compression reduces the amount of data transmitted while allowing the exact original text/characters to be reconstructed; lossy compression permanently discards information and could corrupt names or dates.

Marking: Three valid developed points from no irreversible loss, complete reconstruction, preserved text integrity/accuracy, risks of unreadable or incorrect text under lossy. One idea repeated cannot earn several marks.

RHS ADDITIONAL PRACTICE · QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 3 MARKS · 3 MINUTES

A hospital compresses digital X-ray images before storing them. Explain why lossless compression may be more suitable than lossy compression in this situation. [3]

RHS original follow-on question · linked to this lesson's exam practice

MODEL ANSWER · 3 MARKS

Lossless compression allows the original image to be reconstructed exactly. No image detail is permanently removed, so small features that may be important to a diagnosis are preserved.

Marking: One mark for exact reconstruction, one for no permanent loss of image detail, one for the consequence for medical interpretation.
