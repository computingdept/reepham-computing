Lesson 096 · Little Man Computer: Instructions and the Accumulator

By the end you can…
  • LMC is a teaching model of a stored-program accumulator processor: mailboxes hold instructions or data, and ACC holds the immediate working value.
  • INP replaces ACC; STA copies ACC into a mailbox; LDA copies a mailbox's contents into ACC; ADD adds mailbox contents to ACC.
  • OUT writes current ACC to output. Trace ACC separately from mailbox contents and PC instruction addresses.
