EXAM QUESTION 1 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

A water-level device reads a signed binary floating-point pattern 1010 1110 with four-bit two's-complement fractional mantissa and four-bit two's-complement exponent. The point is immediately after the sign bit. Calculate its denary value with working. [4]

OCR H446/01 · June 2022 · Q3(b) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

Mantissa 1010 = −0.75; exponent 1110 = −2; −0.75 × 2^(−2) = −0.1875 = −3/16.

Marking: Four source-aligned working points: exponent -2; signed mantissa -0.75 / binary-point movement; signed extension or shift; correct -0.1875.

EXAM QUESTION 2 OF 4 · 4 MARKS · 4 MINUTES

A tide-modelling computer needs to store −15.75 in normalised two's-complement floating point. Use exactly EIGHT mantissa bits and FOUR exponent bits, with the binary point immediately after the mantissa sign bit. Show your conversion and both bit fields. [4]

OCR H446/01 · June 2023 · Q3(c) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 4 MARKS

−15.75 = −0.984375 × 2^4. Eight-bit signed fractional mantissa = 10000010; four-bit exponent +4 = 0100. Thus 10000010 0100.

Marking: Four teaching points: signed binary representation, normalising by 4 places, full mantissa 10000010, exponent 0100. Full correct fields plus valid working may earn all four.

EXAM QUESTION 3 OF 4 · 1 MARK · 1 MINUTE

Simplify (NOT A) OR (NOT B) using a De Morgan transformation. [1]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q5(b)(i) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 1 MARKS

NOT (A AND B).

Marking: One mark for equivalent ¬(A ∧ B), correctly grouping both inputs.

EXAM QUESTION 4 OF 4 · 2 MARKS · 2 MINUTES

Use Boolean distribution to simplify (A OR B) AND (A OR C). [2]

OCR H446/01 · June 2024 · Q5(b)(iii) · RHS exam-style scenario

MODEL ANSWER · 2 MARKS

A OR (B AND C).

Marking: One mark for standalone A, one for OR of B AND C. Accept equivalent Boolean syntax.
