I DO · MODELLED

Employee(EmpID,EmpName,DeptID,DeptName) is in 2NF. Why may it violate 3NF?

STEP 1 OF 3

EmpID determines DeptID

Find the given data and name the required result.

STEP 2 OF 3

DeptID determines name

Explain why this intermediate stage is necessary.

STEP 3 OF 3

Split Department

EmpID identifies employee. DeptName depends on DeptID, not directly on EmpID, producing a transitive dependency. Split Employee(EmpID PK,EmpName,DeptID FK) and Department(DeptID PK,DeptName).

WE DO · DIFFERENT CASE

Order(OrderID,CustomerID,CustomerPostcode) stores a customer's postcode on every order. Is a split justified?

STEP 1 OF 3

Order identifies customer

Find the given data and name the required result.

STEP 2 OF 3

Customer identifies postcode

Explain why this intermediate stage is necessary.

STEP 3 OF 3

Split or preserve snapshot

Under the assumption CustomerID determines the customer's current postcode, there is an indirect dependency OrderID→CustomerID→CustomerPostcode. Store Customer(CustomerID PK,Postcode) and Order(OrderID PK,CustomerID FK). Historical shipping addresses may need separate, deliberate snapshots.
