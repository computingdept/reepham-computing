QUESTION 4 OF 4 · YEAR 12 CHALLENGE · LESSON 031 · D-TYPE FLIP-FLOPS

Year 12 challenge

A rising-edge D flip-flop samples D=1 at the first clock edge. D then changes to 0 between edges, and another rising edge occurs. State Q immediately after the first edge, between edges, and after the second edge.

MODEL ANSWER

Q=1 immediately after first rising edge; remains 1 between edges even though D changes; at next rising edge Q becomes 0.

I DO · MODEL

Correct an incomplete two's-complement answer for −9 in eight bits.

Wrong: −9 = 11110111 (no working)
Correct: +9=00001001
invert=11110110; +1=11110111
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Start with +9 in exactly eight bits and invert it.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the answer

Add one to give 11110111, representing −9. This explicitly shows the stages.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

A student writes only '6' for shortest distance A→C using A–B=2, B–C=4, A–C=9. Improve it.

A→B→C costs 2+4=6.
Direct A→C costs 9.
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict first

Compare both candidate routes, recording their total costs.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain the answer

The two-edge route costs 6, which is smaller than the direct route cost 9; explain why.

Foundation · 4 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

Improve an answer to 'trace linear search for 7 in [3,5,7,9]' by showing comparisons and returned index.

WORKED ANSWER

[4] Compare 3 [1], 5 [1], 7 [1], then return index 2 (zero-based) [1].

Core · 6 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

Correct the claim '−6 is 11111001 in eight-bit two's complement'. Provide the full method.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] Positive 6=00000110 [2]; invert to 11111001 [2]; add one to 11111010 [2]. The claimed value is one less and represents −7.

Stretch · 6 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

A student traces binary search for 13 in [1,3,5,7,9,11,13] but does not change high or low after a comparison. Rewrite the trace with bounds and explain why it terminates.

WORKED ANSWER

[6] low0 high6 mid3→7 [1]; target larger so low4 [1]; low4 high6 mid5→11 [1]; increase low6 [1]; mid6→13 found [1]; bounds shrink on each unsuccessful step [1].
