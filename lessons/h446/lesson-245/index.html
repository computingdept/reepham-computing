Simplify F=(¬A ∧ ¬B) ∨ (¬A ∧ B). State the law and final answer.

MODEL ANSWER

Factor ¬A: F=¬A∧(¬B∨B)=¬A∧1=¬A. This also forms a pair of adjacent 1s on a K-map.

Software development methods: evaluation

By the end, you can…
  • Compare methodologies for a specified project
  • Identify impacts of changing requirements and risk
  • Make justified recommendations with drawbacks

NEW LEARNING · CODE FIRST

Predict and explain

// Decision scenario:
// New medical system with uncertain safety risks:
// Spiral emphasises risk assessment per cycle.
// Changing mobile UI with daily user feedback:
// Agile or RAD can be more suitable.
STEP 1 OF 3

Avoid a universal answer

Method suitability depends on risk, requirements, regulation, user access and timescale.

STEP 2 OF 3

Support with scenario evidence

A high-risk system may justify Spiral's explicit risk analysis despite extra overhead.

STEP 3 OF 3

Include a drawback

Evaluate cost, documentation, flexibility and technical quality rather than merely naming a method.

I DO · MODEL

A safety-critical device uses unfamiliar hardware. Evaluate Spiral versus RAD.

Safety risk high → Spiral
Need tested prototypes → cycles of risk review
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict the method

Recommend Spiral because technical risks can be evaluated at each iteration.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and explain

Explain the disadvantage: risk analysis adds project management and cost.

WE DO · DIFFERENT PROBLEM

A small charity wants a booking page within a month and volunteers can review mock-ups daily.

Rapid prototypes + daily feedback
→ RAD approach (with time for testing)
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict the method

RAD fits frequent volunteer feedback and a requirement to develop the interface rapidly.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and explain

Qualify the recommendation: frequent prototyping must not replace security and validation testing.

Foundation · 4 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

Give one defining feature each of Waterfall, Spiral, RAD and XP.

FULL MODEL ANSWER

[4] Waterfall sequential stages [1]; Spiral risk-driven iterations [1]; RAD rapid prototyping with users [1]; XP practices such as TDD/pair programming [1].

Core · 6 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

Choose a development method for a regulated safety-critical control application with uncertain technology. Give three reasons and one drawback.

FULL MODEL ANSWER

[6] Spiral [1]; explicit risk analysis [1]; repeated validation [1]; manage technical uncertainty [1]; formal review [1]; greater cost/overhead [1].

Stretch · 6 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

Evaluate Agile compared with Waterfall for a government reporting system where requirements are partly fixed but user interface needs may change.

FULL MODEL ANSWER

[6] Agile permits feedback and changed UI requirements [2]; Waterfall offers well-defined approval/documentation milestones [2]; propose phased/hybrid governance or justify one method [1]; discuss risk of late change or fixed-scope rigidity [1].
