Today's vocabulary

half adderfull adderSumCarryOutD flip-flop
EXAMPLE FIRST

Read, predict and explain

HALF ADDER:
Sum   = A XOR B
Carry = A AND B

FULL ADDER:
S1 = A XOR B
C1 = A AND B
Sum = S1 XOR CarryIn
C2 = S1 AND CarryIn
CarryOut = C1 OR C2
STEP 1 OF 3

Find the Sum bit

XOR is 1 when A and B are different; A=1, B=1 gives Sum=0.

STEP 2 OF 3

Find the Carry bit

AND is 1 only when both inputs are 1; here Carry=1.

STEP 3 OF 3

Extend to full adder and memory

A full adder also includes CarryIn. Unlike combinational logic, a D flip-flop stores a bit until the triggering clock edge.

I DO · MODEL

A half adder receives A=0, B=1. Calculate Sum and Carry.

Sum = 0 XOR 1 = ?
Carry = 0 AND 1 = ?
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict and explain

XOR(0,1)=1, giving Sum=1.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and extend

AND(0,1)=0, giving Carry=0.

WE DO · DIFFERENT GUIDED PROBLEM

A full adder receives A=1, B=0, CarryIn=1. Calculate outputs.

1 + 0 + 1 = 2 = 10₂
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict and explain

The total is decimal 2, binary 10.

STEP 2 OF 2

Check and extend

Sum is the low bit 0; CarryOut is the high bit 1.

Foundation · QUESTION 1 OF 3

A half adder receives A=1, B=0. Give Sum and Carry.

MODEL ANSWER

Sum=1; Carry=0.

Core · QUESTION 2 OF 3

For a full adder A=1, B=1 and CarryIn=0, calculate Sum and CarryOut.

MODEL ANSWER

1+1+0=2=10₂, so Sum=0, CarryOut=1.

Stretch · QUESTION 3 OF 3

A rising-edge D flip-flop has Q=0 and D=1. D changes to 0 before the next rising clock edge. What value is stored after that edge, and why?

MODEL ANSWER

Q remains 0 because it samples the current D=0 at the rising edge. Changes between edges do not update Q.

TERM 1 OF 5

half adder

Combinational circuit that adds two one-bit inputs.

TERM 2 OF 5

full adder

Adds two bits and a carry-in bit.

TERM 3 OF 5

Sum

Current-column result bit.

TERM 4 OF 5

CarryOut

Bit passed to the next higher column.

TERM 5 OF 5

D flip-flop

Clock-triggered circuit that stores one bit.

True or false?

A D-type flip-flop always changes Q immediately when D changes.

Answer

False. An edge-triggered D flip-flop updates Q only at its triggering clock edge.
