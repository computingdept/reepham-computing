1.4.3(e) · D-TYPE FLIP-FLOP

D is sampled at the clock trigger

Between triggers:
D may change
Q holds

At trigger:
Q ← D
Focus on purpose and clocked behaviour: a D-type flip-flop stores one bit of state.
STEP 1 OF 4

Purpose

stores 1 bit

The flip-flop gives a digital circuit memory.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Inputs

D = data
CLK = clock

D supplies the next state; CLK says when to accept it.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Output

Q = stored state

Q remains unchanged between triggering events.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Registers

8 flip-flops → 8 stored bits

Multiple flip-flops can form registers and other stateful circuits.

4 of 4

Trace three clock triggers

Q starts at 0. Only read D when a trigger occurs.

trigger:   1   2   3

Worked example
STEP 1 · TRIGGER 1

Sample D=1

Q ← 1
STEP 2 · BETWEEN

Ignore D changes

No trigger → Q stays 1
STEP 3 · TRIGGER 2

Sample D=0

Q ← 0
STEP 4 · TRIGGER 3

Sample D=1

Q ← 1

Q sequence: 1, 0, 1
