QUESTION 4 · RANGE & JUSTIFICATION

Store values from 0 to 300

Find the maximum for 8 bits and the minimum width that will store 300.

QUESTION 4 · RANGE & JUSTIFICATION Suggested classroom marks: 4

Choose a storage width

ANSWER
8 bits: 0–255
Minimum for 300: 9 bits
2⁸ − 1 = 255
300 > 255

2⁹ − 1 = 511
9-bit range = 0–511
Suggested marking:
  • 1 mark: 255;
  • 1 mark: explains 300 exceeds 255;
  • 1 mark: 9 bits;
  • 1 mark: justifies with range 0–511.
