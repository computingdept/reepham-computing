Lesson 083 · Dijkstra: Shortest Paths with Non-negative Weights

By the end you can…
  • Dijkstra maintains tentative shortest distances from a start vertex. All edge weights must be non-negative.
  • Repeatedly finalise the unsettled node with smallest tentative distance, then relax outgoing edges to improve neighbour estimates.
  • Track predecessor nodes alongside distances to reconstruct the shortest route, not just its total cost.

I DO · COMPLETE DIJKSTRA TRACE

Find the shortest P→R route. Directed edges: P→Q = 3, P→R = 8, Q→R = 2.

Initial tentative distances
P: 0     Q: ∞     R: ∞
Settled: none
Record each choice, relaxation and predecessor.
STEP 1 · SETTLE P

Update neighbours

Start with dist(P)=0. Settle P (smallest unsettled value). Relax P→Q: Q=3, predecessor Q←P. Relax P→R: R=8, predecessor R←P. Remaining unsettled distances: Q=3, R=8.

STEP 2 · SETTLE Q

Improve the tentative route

Q=3 is the smallest unsettled distance. Via Q→R, the candidate is 3+2=5. This beats R=8, so update R=5 and predecessor R←Q. Settled now: P,Q.

STEP 3 · SETTLE R

Reconstruct the actual path

R=5 is next, and is the target. Follow predecessor pointers R←Q←P; reverse to obtain P→Q→R, total cost 5. No vertex with a larger tentative distance was prematurely finalised.

WE DO · NEW GRAPH

Find shortest X→Z. Directed edges: X→Y = 4, X→Z = 10, Y→Z = 3.

Initial: X=0, Y=∞, Z=∞
Complete:
Settled vertex | dist(Y) | dist(Z) | predecessor of Z
X              |     ?   |     ?   |    ?
Y              |     ?   |     ?   |    ?
Z              |     ?   |     ?   |    ?
STEP 1 · YOUR TURN

Settle X; calculate both tentative distances

Start at X=0. Which neighbour gets distance 4? Which gets 10? Which unsettled vertex should Dijkstra select next?

STEP 2 · CHECK AND CONTINUE

Settle Y and relax Y→Z

After X: Y=4 (predecessor X), Z=10 (predecessor X). Settle Y=4; candidate cost to Z is 4+3=7, so improve Z to 7 with predecessor Y.

STEP 3 · VERIFY

Finish with the path, not just the cost

Settle Z=7. Predecessors: Z←Y←X. Reverse to X→Y→Z, total cost 7. The direct X→Z cost 10 is longer.
