ANSWER

Child data is wrong; use the child index/pointer.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

Retrieval

ANSWER

Stack=FIFO is wrong; stack is LIFO.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Retrieval

Which is wrong: half-adder Sum=AND or Sum=XOR?

ANSWER

Sum=AND is wrong; Sum is XOR.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Challenge

Which is wrong in a K-map: groups of 3 or groups of 4?

ANSWER

Groups of 3 are invalid; use powers of two.
