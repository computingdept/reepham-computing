Lesson 068 · Backtracking: Choices, Dead Ends and Undo

By the end you can…
  • Backtracking explores a choice, tests whether its state remains viable, then undoes the choice if it cannot lead to a solution.
  • A search tree records choices and alternative branches. Pruning avoids expanding branches already proven invalid.
  • A correct search needs a stopping condition. Pruning on sum>target only works when remaining numbers cannot reduce the sum (e.g. non-negative inputs).
