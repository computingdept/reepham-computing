Read the code or problem

8-BIT TWO'S COMPLEMENT
+11 = 00001011
NOT = 11110100
+1  = 11110101
Therefore −11 = 11110101
STEP 1 OF 3

Show positive first

Use exactly eight bits for +11, rather than padding after converting.

STEP 2 OF 3

Invert each bit

00001011 becomes 11110100.

STEP 3 OF 3

Add one

11110100 + 1 = 11110101 for −11.

Timed Q1 · 8 marks · QUESTION 1 OF 3

(a) Represent −19 as an 8-bit two's-complement number. [4] (b) Perform an arithmetic right shift by 1 of your result. State its signed decimal interpretation. [4]

MODEL ANSWER

[8] (a) +19=00010011 [1], invert=11101100 [1], add 1=11101101 [2]. (b) Arithmetic right shift replicates leading 1 [1], gives 11110110 [2], interpreted as −10 [1]; arithmetic right shift of odd negative values rounds towards negative infinity.

Timed Q2 · 8 marks · QUESTION 2 OF 3

(a) Use De Morgan's law to simplify ¬(A ∧ ¬B). [3] (b) Explain how an eight-cell group of ones simplifies a three-variable Karnaugh map. [5]

MODEL ANSWER

[8] (a) ¬A ∨ ¬¬B [2], simplifying to ¬A ∨ B [1]. (b) The group covers all eight 3-variable combinations [2]; all three variables vary [1], leaving no literal [1], so output is constant 1 [1].

Timed Q3 · 8 marks · QUESTION 3 OF 3

A positive two's-complement fractional mantissa is 0.0011₂ with exponent 5. Normalise it, explaining each shift and why the value is unchanged.

MODEL ANSWER

[8] 0.0011×2⁵ → 0.0110×2⁴ [2] → 0.1100×2³ [2]; positive normalised mantissa begins 01 [1]; shifting left doubles mantissa and reducing exponent compensates [2]; decimal value 6 [1].

True or false?

An arithmetic right shift of −19 by one place produces −9.

Model answer

False. It produces −10 in two's complement due to sign extension and rounding towards negative infinity.
