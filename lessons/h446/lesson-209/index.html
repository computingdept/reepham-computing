NEW LEARNING · EXAMPLE FIRST

Predict the effect

+5 = 00000101
NOT=11111010
+1 =11111011 (−5)
0.1100₂ × 2³ = 6₁₀
STEP 1 OF 3

Respect the bit width

Write positive 5 using exactly eight bits.

STEP 2 OF 3

Invert then add one

The result is 11111011₂, representing −5 in 8-bit two's complement.

STEP 3 OF 3

Interpret floating-point differently

0.1100₂ is fractional 0.75; multiplying by 2³ gives 6.

I DO · MODEL

Convert −10 to 8-bit two's complement.

+10=00001010
NOT=11110101
+1=11110110
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

Write 00001010 for +10 and invert to 11110101.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain

Add one: 11110110 represents −10.

WE DO · DIFFERENT GUIDED PROBLEM

Convert −7 to 8-bit two's complement.

+7=00000111
NOT=?
+1=?
STEP 1 OF 2

Predict

Start with +7 = 00000111 and invert all bits.

STEP 2 OF 2

Explain

Add one to 11111000, giving 11111001 for −7.

Foundation · 1 OF 3

Convert unsigned binary 00001011 to decimal.

MODEL ANSWER

11.

Core · 2 OF 3

Encode −12 in 8-bit two's complement.

MODEL ANSWER

+12=00001100, inverted 11110011, add1 = 11110100.

Stretch · 3 OF 3

Normalise positive 0.0011₂ × 2⁵ using a two's-complement fractional mantissa.

MODEL ANSWER

0.0011×2⁵ → 0.0110×2⁴ → 0.1100×2³. Numerical value 6.
