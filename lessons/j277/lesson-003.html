NEW LEARNINGLISTENING

Booklet method: binary first, then two nibbles

STEP 1 · BINARYStart with 128–64–32–16–8–4–2–1 when converting from denary.
STEP 2 · SPLITWrite eight bits as two groups of four (nibbles).
STEP 3 · 8–4–2–1Calculate each nibble separately using 8, 4, 2 and 1.
STEP 4 · HEXReplace nibble values 10–15 with A–F (A=10, B=11, C=12, D=13, E=14, F=15). Hex is a shorter way for people to read binary.
I DOLISTENING

Teacher model

MODEL

Convert 11000111 into hexadecimal.

Use the eight-bit place values: 128 64 32 16 | 8 4 2 1.
Split the number into two nibbles: 1100 | 0111.
Use 8–4–2–1 for each nibble: 8 + 4 = 12; 4 + 2 + 1 = 7.
Look up 12 → C and 7 → 7. Answer: C7.
WE DOMWBs

Guided practice

GUIDED PRACTICE

Convert denary 62 into hexadecimal.

Use the booklet's eight-bit number line: 62 = 32 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2.
Write binary: 00111110. Split into 0011 | 1110.
With 8–4–2–1, the left nibble is 2 + 1 = 3; the right is 8 + 4 + 2 = 14.
Look up 14 → E. Answer: 3E. The alternative division-by-16 method also works.
