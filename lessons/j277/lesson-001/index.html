QUESTION 1 OF 4

Binary digits

Which two digits are used in binary?

ANSWER

0 and 1.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

Byte

How many bits are in one byte?

ANSWER

8 bits.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Place value

What is the value of the left-most column in an unsigned 8-bit number?

ANSWER

128.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Range

What is the largest unsigned value that can be stored in 8 bits?

ANSWER

255.

Eight bits give 256 patterns, from 0 to 255.

Key vocabulary for today
binary denary bit byte place value most significant bit
TERM 1 OF 6

binary

DEFINITION

A base-2 number system using only 0 and 1.

TERM 2 OF 6

denary

DEFINITION

The base-10 number system used in everyday counting.

TERM 3 OF 6

bit

DEFINITION

A single binary digit: 0 or 1.

TERM 4 OF 6

byte

DEFINITION

A group of eight bits.

TERM 5 OF 6

place value

DEFINITION

The value represented by a digit's position.

TERM 6 OF 6

most significant bit

DEFINITION

The highest-value bit in a binary number.
