QUESTION 1 OF 4

Binary → denary

Convert 00101101 from binary to denary.

ANSWER

45

32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45
QUESTION 2 OF 4

Denary → binary

Convert 74 into unsigned 8-bit binary.

ANSWER

01001010

64 + 8 + 2 = 74.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

Nibble

How many bits are in one nibble?

ANSWER

4 bits.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

Reason

Why might programmers prefer hexadecimal to long strings of binary?

ANSWER

It is shorter and easier for humans to read and enter.

Each hexadecimal digit maps directly to four binary bits.

QUICK REFERENCE · 1.2.4aiii & iv

One hexadecimal digit = four binary bits

Binary    Hex
0000       0
0001       1
...
1001       9
1010       A
1011       B
1100       C
1101       D
1110       E
1111       F
Remember: A = 10, B = 11, C = 12, D = 13, E = 14, F = 15.
STEP 1 OF 4

Split binary into nibbles

11000111
→ 1100 0111

Work in groups of four bits from the right.

1 of 4
STEP 2 OF 4

Convert each nibble

1100 = C
0111 = 7
11000111 = C7

Each nibble becomes one hexadecimal digit.

2 of 4
STEP 3 OF 4

Hexadecimal → denary

3E
= (3 × 16) + 14
= 62

The left digit counts sixteens; the right digit counts ones.

3 of 4
STEP 4 OF 4

Denary → hexadecimal

98 ÷ 16 = 6 remainder 2
98 = 62₁₆

Divide by 16: quotient is the first digit, remainder is the second.
