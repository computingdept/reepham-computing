Insertion Sort · starter code

array values[5]
values = [7, 3, 5, 2, 4]

procedure insertionSort()
    for i = 1 to 4
        key = values[i]
        j = i - 1

        while j >= 0 AND values[j] > key
            values[j + 1] = values[j]
            j = j - 1
        endwhile

        values[j + 1] = key
    next i
endprocedure

insertionSort()
for i = 0 to 4
    print(values[i])
next i

STEP 1 OF 5

Begin with one sorted item

The value at index 0 forms a sorted section of one. The outer loop therefore starts at index 1.

1 of 5
STEP 2 OF 5

Save the key

Store values[i] in key before moving other values. Set j to the index immediately to its left.

2 of 5
STEP 3 OF 5

Shift larger values right

While j is valid and values[j] is greater than key, copy that value to j + 1, then move j left.

3 of 5
STEP 4 OF 5

Insert into the gap

The correct gap is j + 1. This also works when j has become -1: insert at index 0.

4 of 5
STEP 5 OF 5

Grow the sorted section

Repeat for the next key. Unlike bubble sort, the stored key is placed after shifting larger values.

5 of 5

Algorithm beside every step
STEP 1 OF 6

Initial sorted section

[7] | 3 5 2 4
Index 0 is treated as sorted.
STEP 2 OF 6

Insert key 3

i = 1; key = 3; j = 0
Shift 7 right: 7 7 5 2 4
j = -1; insert at j + 1 = 0
[3 7] | 5 2 4
STEP 3 OF 6

Insert key 5

i = 2; key = 5; j = 1
Shift 7 right: 3 7 7 2 4
Stop at 3; insert at index 1
[3 5 7] | 2 4
STEP 4 OF 6

Insert key 2

i = 3; key = 2
Shift 7, then 5, then 3 right.
Insert at index 0.
[2 3 5 7] | 4
STEP 5 OF 6

Insert key 4

i = 4; key = 4
Shift 7, then 5 right.
Stop at 3; insert at index 2.
[2 3 4 5 7]
STEP 6 OF 6

Check the invariant

After each outer iteration, indexes 0 through i are in sorted order.
Temporary duplicates during shifting are expected: key preserves the displaced value.
