QUESTION 1 OF 4

From earlier learning

True or false: after completing a Teams assignment, you should choose Hand In.

ANSWER

True. Hand In submits your completed Teams assignment; saving or closing it is not the same as submitting.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

From earlier learning

Which Microsoft app is used to create and use Class Notebook: Notion, OneNote, Evernote or Google Docs?

ANSWER

Microsoft OneNote. Class Notebook is a notebook used in Microsoft Teams / OneNote.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

From earlier learning

Name the four areas shown on the Teams screen in Lesson 3.

ANSWER

Posts, Files, Class Notebook and Assignments. The four areas help you find communication, files, learning and tasks.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

From earlier learning

Challenge: in an email, what is the difference between To, Cc and Bcc?

ANSWER

To: the main recipient(s) who should act. Cc: copies for people kept informed. Bcc: hides recipient addresses from other recipients.

LESSON OBJECTIVES

OneDrive & Class Notebook

7.1 · Introducing Computing · Digital Literacy

By the end of the lesson, I can…

  1. Use OneDrive to create folders and organise my files.
  2. Use Class Notebook to show work from earlier Computing lessons.
  3. Take, crop, copy and paste screenshots into my work.

OneDrive lets you access and edit your files at school and at home.

NEW LEARNING · 1 OF 5

Why use OneDrive?

At school

Open the files saved in your school account.

OneDrive

Online storage keeps your work available across devices.

At home

Sign in with your school account to access and edit the same files.

OneDrive stores and organises files for different subjects. Save schoolwork there rather than relying only on a local My Documents folder.

Class Notebook is used to record learning and show evidence of completed work. Each unit has its own notebook page.

WE DO · GUIDED PRACTICE

Show learning in Class Notebook

OUR SCENARIO

A pupil has completed their email homework and their Teams assignment but hasn't added the evidence to their Class Notebook.

Where should their two screenshots go? What should they do first?

BUILD THE RESPONSE

Our agreed steps

1. Open your Computing Class Notebook.
2. Find the 7.1 Introduction to Computing page. Identify the table where evidence belongs.
3. Add the date before adding any work.
4. Use the Snipping Tool to capture just the relevant part of the example work, then paste it into the correct notebook area.
5. Turn & Talk: how would you prove both pieces of work are complete without showing private information? Count in: 3, 2, 1 — Silence.
TASK 1 · ORGANISE YOUR ONEDRIVE

Create subject folders

Open your school OneDrive and organise it ready for your subjects.

1. Create a folder for Computing.
2. Create folders for your other subjects.
3. Inside Computing, create a folder named 7.1 Introducing Computing.
4. Check that you can open the folders and recognise where work should be saved.

Save your schoolwork in OneDrive so you can access it at school and at home.

TASK 2 · OPEN CLASS NOTEBOOK

Date your unit page

Open the 7.1 Introduction to Computing page in Class Notebook.

1. Open the Computing Class Notebook.
2. Find 7.1 Introduction to Computing.
3. Find the table on that page.
4. Add the date in the correct place.

Check that the date appears on your 7.1 page.

TASK 3 · ADD YOUR EVIDENCE

Paste screenshots of your work

Show the email homework and Teams assignment that you completed in previous lessons.

1. Open the school email you sent as homework.
2. Capture and crop evidence showing the work without private addresses or unrelated messages.
3. Open the Teams assignment you completed.
4. Capture and crop evidence of the assignment.
5. Paste both screenshots into your 7.1 Class Notebook page.

Use the Snipping Tool. Crop your screenshots to show the work, not passwords or private information.

TASK 4 · REVIEW YOUR WORK

Check everything is saved

Check your organisation and evidence before finishing.

1. I can open my OneDrive subject folders.
2. I can find a file, edit it and confirm the changes are saved.
3. My Class Notebook page contains today's date.
4. My screenshots show the email and Teams work clearly and safely.

Check that your files are saved in OneDrive and your evidence appears in Class Notebook.
