SLANT lesson behaviour

S — Sit Up
Sit up straight, facing forward, ready to learn.
L — Listen Carefully
Pay attention when others are speaking and follow instructions.
A — Ask and Answer
Ask questions and contribute answers.
N — Never Interrupt
Let others finish speaking before you talk.
T — Track the Speaker
Keep your eyes on the person who is talking.

Safe and responsible use

Filtering

If you try to open a website the school considers inappropriate, access may be blocked. If you think a website should or should not be blocked, tell a teacher.

Monitoring

Staff can see school device screens, internet history, files and emails. School devices and accounts should only be used for schoolwork.

Important

Breaking the Acceptable Use Agreement in lessons or through monitoring can lead to a negative on Arbor. Two negatives trigger a lunchtime detention.
