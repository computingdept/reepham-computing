1. Give one rule that helps everyone learn in a computer room.

Example: listen when someone is speaking, support others, or keep food and drink away from the desks.

2. What should you do when someone else is speaking?

Listen and let them finish before you talk.

3. Why should food and drink be kept away from computer desks?

It helps protect the equipment and keeps the room safe and tidy.

4. Challenge: Why might a school block some websites?

To stop students opening websites the school thinks are not suitable.

Work standards — open when you need them
  • Add the date to each Class Notebook row.

Catching up? Click on the right side of the screen to reveal each check. Never type your real school password into this page.

Step 1 — Count it.
It has more than 10 characters.
Step 2 — Check it is not obvious.
It is not just a first name or a simple number.
Step 3 — Keep it private.
Do not tell friends your password. Do not use someone else's login.

Teacher model

Ask:

  • Is it long enough?
  • Would it be easy for someone else to guess?
  • Will I keep it private?
