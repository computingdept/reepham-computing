Can school email be used for personal messages?

ANSWER

No. School email should be used for school purposes.

QUESTION 2 OF 4

From previous learning

Name the four main parts of a formal email.

ANSWER

Subject, greeting, body and sign-off.

QUESTION 3 OF 4

From previous learning

Give two ways to make a strong school password.

ANSWER

Use a sufficiently long password that is hard to guess and avoid obvious personal information. Follow school password requirements.

QUESTION 4 OF 4

From previous learning

Why might someone use Bcc when emailing several families?

ANSWER

Bcc prevents recipients seeing one another’s email addresses, protecting privacy.

NEW LEARNING · 1 OF 3

What is Microsoft Teams?

A Team is a space where a class can communicate, work together and find learning resources. Your teachers may set assignments in Teams for you to complete.

Used in Computing and other subjects, at school and at home.

WE DO · GUIDED REHEARSAL

What happens next?

OUR SCENARIO

Alex has opened the homework assignment in Teams but has not submitted it.

In pairs, describe how Alex should finish the work and ensure the teacher receives it.

BUILD THE RESPONSE

Our agreed steps

1. First, open the correct assignment in your class Team.
2. Next, read the instructions and complete Task 1 and Task 2.
3. Then, check that the file or answer has been saved or attached as required.
4. Finally, choose Hand In and confirm that Teams shows the work as submitted.
5. Turn & Talk: explain why just closing the assignment is not enough. Teacher counts in: 3, 2, 1 — Silence.

True or false?

You must press Hand In for Teams to record a completed assignment as submitted.

Raise your hand for your answer when the teacher asks. Explain why.

EXIT TICKET · ANSWER 1 OF 3

Check your thinking

You must press Hand In for Teams to record a completed assignment as submitted.

True

Hand In submits the work; merely opening or editing it is not a submission.

EXIT TICKET · ANSWER 2 OF 3

Check your thinking

Shared files and Assignments always mean exactly the same thing in Teams.

False

Shared is where class files can be found, while Assignments is used for tasks set by teachers.
