I Do
To

The main recipient. They are the person expected to read or act on the email.

Cc

Sends a copy to someone who needs to be kept informed.

Bcc

Hides recipients' email addresses from one another, helping protect privacy.

Pre-send check

  • I chose the correct recipient.
  • My subject tells the reader what the email is about.
  • I used a polite greeting.
  • My body is clear and uses full sentences.
  • I used an appropriate sign-off.
  • I reread the email before sending.
  • I only used Cc/Bcc if there is a clear reason.
  • I did not include private information unnecessarily.
One real email is enough. Your teacher will tell you the recipient and purpose. The website practice lets you rehearse the other examples without filling everyone's inbox.

Presentation

Computing work is usually presented clearly

  • Use black or blue ink for written work.
  • Cross out mistakes with one clear line.
  • Use capital letters and punctuation.
  • Use full sentences when appropriate.
  • Keep pages in order and in good condition.
  • For digital work, use clear headings and crop screenshots neatly.
