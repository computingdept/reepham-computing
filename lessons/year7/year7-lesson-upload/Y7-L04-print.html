New Learning

Why use OneDrive?

OneDrive is online storage for your files. It helps you organise school work and access it from home and school.

Good habit: Store school work in your OneDrive so you can find it again on different school devices and at home.

I Do

What is Class Notebook for?

Each unit can have a page in your Class Notebook. You can use it to keep notes and evidence of your work so your teacher can check your understanding, effort and presentation.

Screenshot

Capture just the evidence you need

Windows + Shift + S

Choose the area you need, then paste the screenshot into Class Notebook. Crop it so the important work is easy to see.

You Do

Add evidence from your first lessons

  • Open the 7.1 Introduction to Computing page in Class Notebook.
  • Add the date to the correct row or section.
  • Add a screenshot of the email you sent for Lesson 2/homework.
  • Add a screenshot of the Teams assignment you completed in Lesson 3.
  • Crop screenshots so they show the work clearly.
  • Check that your evidence is readable before you finish.
Privacy: do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information in a screenshot.
