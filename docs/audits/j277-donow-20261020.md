# J277 whole-course Do Now update — 20 October 2026

All 164 numbered lessons use four questions. Q1 is short previous-lesson recall (KS3 baseline in Lesson 001). Q2 is always four-option, single-answer multiple choice. Q3 retrieves earlier subject learning. Q4 applies earlier learning through reasoning, worked examples, traces or misconception analysis.

## Authority and coverage

`lessons/j277/donow-bank-20261020.json` is the authoritative whole-course question bank (656 original questions, including 164 MCQs). Its questions include options, correct letters, answers, explanations, specification mappings, source lessons and allocation IDs. The old `r` arrays in lesson source files are retained only as historical/fallback data; the J277 loaders install the bank before rendering.

`j277-donow-coverage-20261020.json` records all 221 planning checks under the 89 main content bullets. Each check has three allocated appearances in separate lessons after its teaching point. Closely related checks share 74 explicitly paired questions. A paired question must address both checks. Recognition in an MCQ is a retrieval exposure, not evidence of independent mastery. Some planning checks intentionally group related operations; the scope of each check is recorded. The 221 count is a planning decomposition, not an official OCR count.

Second appearances have gaps of at least eight numbered lessons; third appearances have gaps of at least ten. The recorded intervals are lessons, not calendar weeks. Source benchmark: Cambridge OCR J277 specification version 3.1 (May 2026), https://www.ocr.org.uk/Images/558027-specification-gcse-computer-science-j277.pdf .
