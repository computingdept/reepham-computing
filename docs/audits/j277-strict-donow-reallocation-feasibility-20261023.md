# J277 — strict four-question Do Now reallocation: feasibility and limits

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** Source-derived feasibility only. **No student-facing Do Now, teaching lesson, Code First, JS/CSS or H446 files modified.**

## Constraint to solve

- **164 lessons × four Do Now slots = 656 questions.** Q1 must recall the previous lesson; Q2 must remain one short A–D MCQ; Q3 retrieves earlier teaching; Q4 poses one challenging application, not two independent '(a)/(b)' questions.
- **221 local specification planning checks × three spaced Do Now appearances = 663 awarded credits.** The 221 checks are an internal teaching/audit decomposition of OCR J277, not an official OCR count. Thus at least **seven question positions must genuinely evidence two checks**, even if every other slot counts.
- A prior audit found **196 checks at 3, 25 below** in the current recorded coverage map (620 allocated credits), then drafted 31 Q4 clarity corrections: **21 cohesive integrated tasks provisionally retain two credits**, **10 single-skill tasks deliberately lose one of their prior two credits**. Applying the latter yields **610 credits**, with **53 missing**.
- The 21 integrated two-check Q4s are **provisional** and only defensible where the single question and mark scheme demonstrably assess both check descriptions; they are not rubber-stamped.

## Candidate Q1 reassignment proof

We screened all **52 Q1 records currently without an approved allocated check** against their already-authored questions, previous-lesson topic labels, the fine-grained check's planned teaching index, assessment exclusions, and existing spaced allocations. We did not rewrite these Q1 questions.

- **36 feasible Q1 accreditation moves** under a conservative screening procedure.
- Of these, **35 move an already credited check from a later Q2/Q3/Q4 to a suitable previous-lesson Q1**, freeing that old Q2/Q3/Q4 for a new independent retrieval question.
- **One is a direct added Q1 credit** on a check below three. Under-covered checks can still need additional encounters even after that change.
- The bank also has **11 uncredited Q2/Q3/Q4 slots in lessons 161–164**. Earlier blank slots are KS3 / early topic anchors and cannot simply be reclassified against later-taught OCR subrequirements.
- **Conservative upper bound:** 35 released slots + 11 later blank slots = **46 available Q2/Q3/Q4 placements** after Q1 moves, whereas **52 additional credits remain needed**. **At least six further credits remain structurally unplaced** under these assumptions (fixed 21 integrated Q4s, 10 single-check Q4s, existing Q1 wording and all stated prerequisites). This is **not** a universal impossibility proof: legitimate changes to some other single-task Q4s, a deeper redistribution of Q1, or a broader definition of retrieval exposure could alter the result.
- Refer to [36 Q1 relocation candidates](j277-strict-donow-36-q1-relocation-candidates-20261023.csv). **None is applied.**

## Feasibility matching of the 46 candidate Q2–Q4 locations

A conservative, deterministic matching search was run against all **32 checks still below three after the simulated Q4 and Q1 moves**. Each proposed placement:
- occurs after the check's planned teaching index,
- is a distinct lesson,
- respects the planned gap of **at least eight lesson positions** for the second appearance and **ten for the third**,
- sits in a pre-existing Q2/Q3/Q4 position freed by a specific Q1 swap or in a formerly uncredited final-course position,
- avoids adding new questions to bespoke assessment lesson positions.

**One feasible, not necessarily optimal match assigns 43 new credits** in those locations and fully restores 29 of the 32 remaining deficit checks. It uses 43/46 available positions; three early free positions (L009 Q2, L010 Q4, L025 Q2) cannot help the remaining later-taught topic checks. That leaves **nine planned credits missing in this illustrative match**, in three previously zero-coverage checks:

But the nine selected positions overlap with earlier proposals and have **not** been jointly optimised with the other 43 placements. The 43-item match should **not be published** with all three zero-checks unresolved.

## What this means for the 53 editorially reviewed drafts

The 53 supplemental retrieval questions and 31 Q4 rewrites are fully authored as **unpublished audit materials**, but the 53 cannot simply be placed as extra Do Now questions without breaking the four-slot routine or losing old credit. Importing them as separate revision checkpoints is useful **additional retrieval exposure**, not a literal three Do Now appearances.

**The previous simulation of 221/221 three-pass coverage was for independent supplemental checkpoints**, not a validated Do Now-only solution. Keep this distinction explicit.

## Recommended next corrective design step

1. **Prioritise five originally zero-credit checks**, especially the three that the current matched plan leaves empty. Plan the nine zero-first prompts so they do not displace other credits without recovery.
2. Identify **at least six, and potentially more, valid additional integrated Q4 credit opportunities** *only where one authentic scenario or comparison actually assesses two closely related requirements*. Do not reintroduce a two-part Q4 merely to meet a numerical target.
3. Rerun a **joint, exact allocation solver** for all 221 checks, including original 4-slot limits, Q1 previous-lesson requirement, Q2 simple MCQ, lesson prerequisites, 8/10 spacing, bespoke assessments and new Q4 integrations. A greedy 43-credit match is evidence of feasibility for those specific positions, **not** a global optimum.
4. Draft actual short four-choice Q2 items (each with one focus, four short distinct options and one correct letter), and genuine challenging Q4 problems with separate mark schemes. Cross-check all source/answer paths.
5. Only after a complete verified Do Now reallocation, stage **J277-only** publication and check desktop 1920×1080 and mobile around 390×844. Do not touch H446/shared teaching framework.
