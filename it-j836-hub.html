Information Technology

132 lesson positions

Year 10 lessons

This plan uses a January Year 11 terminal route: Year 10 is 001–095 and Year 11 is 096–132. The qualification has 120 guided learning hours, so the extra positions give space for assessment, catch-up and exam revision.

R060

Spreadsheet skills

Use formulas, functions, data tools, charts and testing to solve a set problem.

R070

AR product skills

Plan, build, test and review an augmented reality product for a set brief.

R050

IT in the digital world

Practise data, cyber security, digital systems, planning and real-world IT scenarios.

R050 must be taken in the final assessment series. R060 and R070 must be submitted in the same series or an earlier one.
